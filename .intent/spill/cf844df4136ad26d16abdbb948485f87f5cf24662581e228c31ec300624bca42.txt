36: ## Purpose
37: 
38: Apeiron is one R0 mathematical/computational foundation: canonical Lean and reusable Rust in one
39: target repository, with Song/Slang as independent execution sharing the mathematics.
40: Hashtrinity/fold is its historical implementation; the two-slot spinor and affine recursion collapse
41: are its semantic law; the Table ordered fold is its state/fold form; SKI/derived-Iota supplies its
42: normal form/translation; Rust and Song/Slang are concrete realizations of that primitive.
43: These are layers/views of one primitive, not separate foundations or programme tracks. This is the
44: architecture requirement, not a claim that the complete connecting theorem already exists.
45: 
46: Apeiron evolves from hashtrinity; `fold/hashtrinity` is the predecessor. The basis is
47: `R[x]/(x^2 - sigma)`, `Cell<R>` with explicit `Sigma`, and face-correct `Coord` addressing.
48: The executing [src/table.rs](../src/table.rs) is already growable: a flat certified-coordinate
49: resident area plus grouped spill regions, keyed by `(Sigma,K)`, with `Stored`/`Regen` carriers.
50: `SizeOverflow` and `AllocationFailed` are resource outcomes; `ShardFull` is compatibility-only.
51: Implementation presence does not close the extraction/refinement integration wave. The earlier
52: fixed-shard layout and its bounded-admission proofs are historical, not proofs of this layout.
53: Every executing face must refine the
54: canonical object; anything not rooted in it is not accepted. The canonical object is
55: `Apeiron.Table.{HMap,get,insertWith,build}` and
56: `Apeiron.Trichotomy.AnyRing.{RingCell,ringMul,ringNormForm}` with the extracted `Cell<i64>` word
57: bodies (`word_mul_denote`, `word_norm_denote`).
58: 
59: Name the algebra and its faces by their standard mathematical names and notation: `R[x]/(x²−σ)`;
60: elliptic (`σ<0`), parabolic (`σ=0`), hyperbolic (`σ>0`); the norm form, idempotents and zero divisors
61: keep their usual names. Cite the originator(s) of a borrowed object before its reference, and
62: retrieve the reference before calling it evidence. Legacy coinages are not reused for new objects.
63: 
64: Fold/hashtrinity -> Apeiron consolidation must be **performance-preserving**: it carries forward
65: the high-performance implementation's useful tested surfaces, not merely its names or formulas.
66: The selected production primitive must both refine canonical `Apeiron.Table` / `AnyRing` and meet
67: the performance restoration gate below. Extraction convenience cannot justify replacing a
68: materially faster implementation with a slower structure. The central proof obligation is the
69: canonical denotation of the performance-bearing executing implementation; empirical confidence of
70: the SwissTable/hashbrown kind alone is insufficient. Proof and performance are joint G1 acceptance:
71: neither proof without performance nor speed without canonical refinement passes.
72: 
73: The earlier O(1)-at-fixed-N qualifier belongs to fixed-capacity primitives with bounded callbacks,
74: not to the growable table: a fixed probe-group width does not bound the number of groups searched.
75: Reading a whole source remains at least linear in its bytes; no constant-time whole-source checking
76: or constant-size proof term follows. Resource accounting remains implementation-specific; this qualification
77: does not replace the required collapse/fold correspondence or the G1/REG-1 performance gate below,
78: including restoration of historical performance.

100: ## Thesis
101: 
102: The basis is `R[x]/(x^2 - sigma)`: one two-slot algebra, with elliptic, parabolic and hyperbolic
103: regimes over the reals. The chosen scalar and each theorem's hypotheses remain explicit. The goal
104: is to construct computation, state, transformations and their verification from this basis and use
105: them in literal machines. The scientific thesis, "physics == computation", motivates the physical
106: models; it is not a claim that every physical interpretation is already proved.
107: 
108: Machine words, cells, combinator terms and proof terms have distinct representations and roles.
109: Each executing implementation requires its own refinement to the relevant canonical definition.
110: Song's independent implementation is intentional; an unproved substitute presented as a refinement
111: is not. Canonical mathematical content stays in Lean alongside Apeiron's Rust implementation.
112: 
113: The primary system deliverable is a secure kernel with a coupled language and proof foundation:
114: memory and authority safety follow from the admitted representations and checked transitions, not
115: merely from an optional checker applied to otherwise unrelated code. This is the system to build,
116: not a claim that the present components already establish its security.
117: 
118: Each refinement is directional and explicit. A representation theorem links one concrete layout to
119: the canonical object; a second refinement links another layout to the same object. The canonical
120: composition theorems are then reused by both, rather than re-derived per layout. Whether that reuse
121: reduces total effort is a measurable programme goal, not an assumption.
122: 
123: A table state denotes a function; an update denotes a function update; a carrier step denotes a ring
124: operation. Those denotations are stated per implementation against the canonical definitions, and
125: the representation and admission relation of each implementation is proved separately.
126: 
127: The programme goal is a single R0 chain through the same canonical collapse/fold object:
128: 
129: ```text
130: lift (source / LLVM)
131:   -> canonical collapse / ordered fold (two-slot algebra and spinor law)
132:   -> SKI / derived-Iota normal form / translation and checked reduction / subsumption
133:   -> actual realization (Rust and independent Song / Slang execution)
134:   -> safe and useful programmes
135: ```
136: 
137: Local algebra, matrix, Cayley, online-chart, table and evaluator proofs close only their named
138: layer until a checked theorem connects the complete hashtrinity/fold/spinor/SKI/Slang chain.
139: Historical recurrence-to-constant-loop and evaluator-speed anchors remain anchors, not proofs or
140: measurements. An admitted normal-form/translation outcome does not assert that every SKI term
141: normalizes or that every implementation runs in constant time.
142: 
143: Terms are used precisely:
144: 
145: - **Entry encoding vs semantic lift.** Translating a source or compiler artifact into a proof
146:   assistant is an entry encoding. A semantic lift additionally relates the artifact's actual
147:   behaviour to a canonical definition on a matched domain. Extraction alone is an entry encoding.
148: - **Syntax, convertibility, route and observable equalities.** Syntactic equality, convertibility,
149:   equality of a reduction route and equality of observable results are different relations. A
150:   verdict that decides one does not silently decide another.
151: - **Lossless reconstruction vs observable relative subsumption.** Reconstructing the original
152:   program exactly is stronger than showing a transformed program is observationally equivalent on a
153:   stated set of observations. The weaker relation is admissible only when its observation set,
154:   refusal and resource behaviour are stated.
155: - **Code, data and runtime cost vs actual computer work.** Bytes of source, bytes moved and
156:   wall-clock time are different quantities. A cost claim names the metric, the machine and the
157:   interval.

159: ## Lineage: the fold crossover
160: 
161: The fold crosses from hashtrinity into apeiron, and that crossover is the lineage.
162: `HashTrinity/Core.lean` (`insertWith`, `build`, `get_build_acc`) and `Song/Table.lean` (`put`,
163: `run`, `merge_is_state_fold`) are the same total map, new-first update and ordered build fold as
164: canonical `Apeiron.Table`; `hashtrinity/src/algebra.rs` supplies the predecessor
165: `Coord`/`CoordInv`/`Stored`/`Regen`, folded multiplication and the four merges. Apeiron changes that
166: lineage by separating exact packing from lossy coordinates, repairing the unguarded byte fold, and
167: using bounded home-shard storage with explicit refusal; canonical
168: `Table.Basic.build_gauge_invariance_mul`, `AnyRing.ringMul_comm` and `AnyRing.ringMul_assoc` close
169: the algebraic side. An audited cross-revision equivalence artifact is still missing, so a
170: byte-hash-equals-`ringMul` claim or an arbitrary-merge classification is not made.

172: ## Ownership
173: 
174: - **Apeiron** owns the canonical definitions, the reusable Rust primitives and their refinements.
175:   Lean is retained: the canonical mathematics, its history and its attribution stay in the tree.
176:   Reusable Rust and refinements converge in Apeiron. The approved destination is Apeiron, not a
177:   sibling tree. Source preservation targets a canonical package that is independent of any single
178:   Aeneas extraction link.
179: - **Song** remains an independent, adjacent assembly-up implementation: its own assembly, Slang
180:   implementation, contracts and local build/runtime acceptance. Where Song is slower, it derives its
181:   approaches from Apeiron; Apeiron's proven core is the reference. There is no Rust or Aeneas
182:   runtime linkage between Apeiron and Song, and Lean is not deleted. Song's local gates stay owned
183:   by Song. The required cross-repo result is a proof of primitive correspondence, not runtime
184:   linkage.
185: - **The substrate is shared, and Song's data operations run on it.** Song MUST carry the canonical
186:   Apeiron table and MUST route its data operations through it. A private probe, lookup or merge in
187:   Song that the canonical table already supplies is a **rebasing obligation, not an alternative
188:   implementation**: the duplicate is the defect, the canonical object is the replacement. This is
189:   not a trade between hard-and-good and easy-and-bad — the shared substrate *is* the architecture,
190:   and a second implementation of it is not the cheap route. Song predates Apeiron, so the rebasing is
191:   real work with an order. Where Song's architecture cannot host the canonical table, **Song's
192:   architecture changes**; the requirement is not discharged by keeping the duplicate. Local gates
193:   stay owned by Song; convergence tasks are cross-repo tasks closed by Song's own gates.
194: - **Applications** retain local policy. Penglai owns fiction, materials' physical interpretation,
195:   gameplay policy and host integration; Lyrium extracts engine machinery later; Fold is not a
196:   consumer or requirements source; Whispering Tides owns game and scientific conformance, not
197:   network. Application Rust and game policy remain local.
198: - The central programme coordinates named cross-repo tasks. Local owners retain implementation
199:   detail and gates; they may not silently remove a programme outcome or change the shared boundary.
200: - The physical canonical corpus is still `../sanguine-apeiron/proof/Apeiron/`. Apeiron is the
201:   approved destination, but the sources have not moved and no pin changes in this pass. See

203: - All AI-written code is written under user direction. Commit authors and optional trailers are not

408: ## Primitive correspondence
409: 
410: Primitive correspondence is the required cross-repo result. It relates two executing
411: implementations — the Rust primitive in Apeiron and the Slang/assembly primitive in Song — to the
412: same canonical definition on a matched domain. The obligations are joint, not separate:
413: 
414: 1. **Representation.** Each concrete layout denotes the canonical object, with the denotation
415:    stated and its domain explicit.
416: 2. **State and effects.** Each implementation's state transition denotes the canonical transition;
417:    side effects and ordering are stated where they exist.
418: 3. **Admission, success and refusal.** The profile states the matched semantic domain and each
419:    implementation's admission and resource bounds. On success the implementation preserves the
420:     canonical meaning. The profile specifies unsupported-input and resource-exhaustion behavior,
421:     including whether state or effects can change on refusal. Where nonmutation is part of the
422:     primitive contract, state preservation must be proved. A defined canonical value may still be
423:     refused when an implementation lacks capacity; the domain is not silently reduced and no
424:     canonical partiality is fabricated.
425: 4. **Resources.** Cost and resource bounds are stated per implementation and are not silently
426:    identified across layouts.
427: 
428: Equal names, worked examples, citations or identical layouts are not that proof. Rust and Slang have
429: different physical layouts: Rust executes the growable flat/spill `Table`; Song's Slang/assembly
430: representation is its own. The historical fixed-shard proof does not discharge the new Rust layout.
431: Different layouts can correspond, but only under a matched admission relation and the four
432: obligations above. A differential test or a shared example
433: is supporting evidence, not a substitute for the correspondence theorem.
434: 
435: This correspondence is a required programme outcome and is not established today. See the
436: [local boundary](kernel-boundary.md) for the Rust-side representation and trust inventory.
437: 
438: Correspondence is directional and explicit. A refinement may prove that implementation A refines the
439: canonical definition; a second refinement proves that implementation B refines the same definition.
440: The correspondence between A and B is then the composite, and it is checked, not assumed from shared
441: naming or a shared test suite. This is why the canonical definition must be the shared object, and
442: why an independently written implementation needs a checked refinement rather than a claim of
443: equivalence. The artifacts remain distinct; their canonical semantic authority is shared.
