## Result

A 1-bit ternary weight read (two `popc.b64` per 64 terms) runs at **539.5 GB/s**, 87.6% of the
616.0 GB/s spec peak (2 × 7.000 GHz × 352 bit ÷ 8). The 7B pack's 0.998 GB/token therefore has a
**540.5 tok/s** bandwidth ceiling on this card: **27.0× the 20 tok/s target**, and 20 tok/s would
consume 3.7% of the measured bandwidth.

| measurement | bytes/dispatch | median ms | spread ms | GB/s | tok/s |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `popcount_bw`, 1 GiB stream | 1,073,741,824 | 1.9902 | 1.9867–1.9927 | **539.5** | 502.5 |
| `ternary_gemv`, m=3584 k=18944 | 8,486,912 | 0.0250 | 0.0246–0.0264 | 339.5 | 40,013 |
| `ternary_fold`, m=3584 groups=34816 | 998,244,352 | 1.8588 | 1.8412–1.8764 | **537.0** | 538.0 |

Medians are over the three run medians below; each run is 5 warmup dispatches then 5 timed
dispatches, timed with `cuEventElapsedTime` around one dispatch. Spread is the union of the three
runs' `[min..max]` over their timed five. `tok_s` is one dispatch per token, so for the two GEMV
rows it is a pack rate; the 1 GiB stream row's 502.5 is one token of 1 GiB, not a pack rate.

The 8.49 MB GEMV row is a shape and parallelism probe, not a DRAM number: the launch is 3584
warps (448 blocks of 256), so it measures what this shape's occupancy sustains, not the card's
streaming peak. The 0.998 GB fold is the DRAM number, and it agrees with the independent 1 GiB
stream to 0.5% (537.0 against 539.5 GB/s), so the carrier's read rate is not sensitive to the
access pattern or the row mapping.

Writes are excluded from the rate: the stream writes 2,097,152 B of per-warp sums (0.2%), the fold
557,056 B of activation masks and 14,336 B of output (0.06%).

### Ceilings

`bandwidth ÷ bytes-per-token`, with the measured bandwidth, not the spec peak:

| pack | GB/token | ceiling tok/s | vs 20 tok/s | fraction of bandwidth at 20 tok/s |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7B 1-bit, 0.998244352 GB (measured stream) | 0.998244352 | **540.5** | 27.0× headroom | 3.70% |
| 7B 1-bit, 0.998244352 GB (measured fold) | 0.998244352 | 538.0 | 26.9× headroom | 3.72% |
| 7B Q4, 4.3706 GB/token (arithmetic only) | 4.3706 | 123.4 | 6.2× headroom | 16.2% |

The Q4 row is arithmetic from the same 539.5 GB/s, not a Q4 measurement. No 0.5B pack was run, so
no 0.5B ceiling is quoted; a 0.5B number here would be a model, not this receipt's evidence.

The binding constraint at 20 tok/s is therefore not weight bandwidth on this GPU: 3.7% of the
measured stream leaves 96% of the card for attention, KV traffic and the head. What this does not
establish is that the rest of the pipeline fits in that budget — only that the carrier read does.

## Method

`ternary_gemv`: one warp per row, lane `l` strides groups by 32, `popc.b64` on `w & P[g]` and
`w & N[g]`, `shfl.sync.down.b32` warp reduction, lane 0 stores an f32. 3584 warps = 448 blocks ×
256 threads. Accumulators are i32: a lane sees at most ⌈34816/32⌉ = 1088 groups of 64 terms, so a
lane partial is ≤ 69,632 and a row sum ≤ 2,225,152, both exact in f32.

`popcount_bw`: grid-stride over 2^27 u64 words, warp-reduced popcount per 32 words, one u32 store
per warp.

The ternary arithmetic is `Σ wᵢaᵢ` with `w ∈ {0,1}`, `a ∈ {-1,0,+1}`, encoded as positive mask `P`
and negative mask `N`: `popcount(w & P) − popcount(w & N)`. That is two popcounts per 64 terms.
The CPU cell's four-popcount form uses a different bit encoding, so this is not that cell; the
bytes per term, which bind here, are the same.

Result checks run before any timing, and the program exits 1 on a mismatch:

- `ternary_gemv_check rows=4 bad=0` — rows 0, 1, 1792, 3583 against a CPU `__builtin_popcountll`
  evaluation of the uploaded bytes.
- `ternary_fold_check rows=2 bad=0` — rows 0 and 3583 of the 0.998 GB fold. The fold's weights are
  a known `0x5a` byte pattern (every word `0x5a5a5a5a5a5a5a5a`), so both rows are checkable
  against the CPU without a 1 GB host copy.

All three runs printed `bad=0` on both checks; the checks are exact integer identities, not
tolerances.

## What this does not cover

- **The repository's Vulkan/SPIR-V kernel.** No `src/gpu` kernel executed. V1 is not advanced; the
  numbers are a CUDA probe of the same carrier on the same silicon.
- **The 1-bit carrier as implemented code.** `src/gpu` has no 1-bit ternary kernel; this probe
  writes the encoding to measure its bytes, not to match a repo body. The two-popcount form is
  not the crate's four-popcount cell.
- **A model.** No full decode, no attention, no KV cache, no output head, no tokenizer, no
  sampling. One projection shape's weight traffic, repeated.
- **Quality.** No PPL, no Q4_K_M comparison, no real weights; the weights are synthetic and the
  activation masks random. Popcount is data-independent, so the byte rate carries over, but
  nothing about accuracy does.
- **Prefill.** `k > 1` batching was not measured; every dispatch is a single-token fold.
- **The 7B pack's real geometry.** `m=3584, groups=34816` is chosen so the dispatch reads exactly
  0.998 GB, not to match an NQ pack's per-layer allocation.
- **A solo card.** The GPU is fractional (`gpu_frac 0.125`), time-shared with other tenants, so
  87.6% of spec is a lower bound under contention, not the card's ceiling on an idle host.
- **rustc, LLVM, the kernel ABI, the Vulkan driver itself, and the `unsafe` container.** Outside
  this probe.
- **Cost control.** $0.10222/h for 0.38 h on this host; $0.2396 across all four rented hosts,
  every instance destroyed (see below).
