## 2. Local run under a 3.6 GiB scope: GO

File pages are charged to the cgroup when touched but are clean and reclaimable, so `MemoryMax`
forces eviction, not an OOM kill; `MemorySwapMax=0` keeps the anon set out of swap.

| term | bytes | grade |
|---|---:|---|
| KV, 36 layers × 8 kv heads × 128 × 2 (k,v) × 4 B × 2048, `Carrier::F32` (`attn.rs:131`, `mixer.rs:321`) | 603,979,776 | `model` from source |
| logits, 16 × 151,936 × 8 B (Q32.32 `i64`) | 19,447,808 | `model` |
| prefill states, 2048 × 4096 × 8 B | 67,108,864 | `model` |
| **anonymous total** | **~0.72 GiB** | `model` |
| GGUF file pages | 5,027,783,488 | `measured` (size) |

`MemoryMax=3600M` leaves ~2.8 GiB of page cache for a 4.68 GiB file: ~1.8 GiB is re-read per
trunk pass. `prefill_states` walks the trunk once per block pass (`step.rs:2008`), not once per
token, so the wall is compute-bound first and I/O-bound second.

NVMe sequential read, `dd iflag=direct`, two 512 MiB runs at offsets 2.4 and 3.2 GiB:
**1.9 GB/s** *(m)*. A pathological 5.03 GB re-read per token is 2.65 s/token, **1.5 h** for 2,048
tokens, not the 10 h a 279 MB/s first-touch read suggested (that figure was one cold 256 MiB run
and is not the drive's steady rate). Expected wall 20–60 min *(a)*; abort at 3 h.

GO, and only under `MemoryMax=3600M`, `MemorySwapMax=0`, `APEIRON_THREADS=4`, with no other heavy
job. **The one blocker left is the 4.33 GiB download** (section 3); the unblock is the verified pair.

`bench/cpu/1794912865-nq8b-overnight.md` prices the KV at 302,776,320 B (2 B/word); the default
carrier is `F32` over a `u32` word, so 604 MB funds this run. The T1 bytes/token row is 302 MB low.
