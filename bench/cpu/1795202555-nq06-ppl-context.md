# The 0.6B dial at 0.977 b/w rises from 74 to 572 PPL as the window grows

Criterion: **Q2** (quality) for the PPL rows and the rate; **C2** for the context
behaviour. Grade: `measured` for every PPL and for the pre-S1 identity; `model` for the attribution
of the rise to the pack's long-range attention; `anchor` for the cited 8B rows, which were not re-run
here. Machine: ThinkPad L13 Yoga 20R6, i5-10210U (4 cores / 8 threads), 6.6 GiB, `performance`,
`APEIRON_THREADS=4`, pinned to CPUs 0,1,3,4 of a host whose CPU 6 held a concurrent `node` at ~90%.

## Result

One pack (`nq06-v3.nqp`, sha256 `2e4670a4…36e6`, 0.977441 b/w), one token stream (raw wikitext-2
test, first 2,048 tokens), one carrier (F32), three window lengths. The windows are the same first
2,048 tokens; only the attention span differs. The current tree (`c38b8c14`) and the pre-S1 tree
(`fd6ad4b0`) return the same words to the last digit:

| ctx | windows | scored | current PPL | mean nll | pre-S1 PPL | mean nll |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 512 | 4 | 2,044 | **74.296766** | 4.308067 | 74.296766 | 4.308067 |
| 1,024 | 2 | 2,046 | **111.066967** | 4.710133 | — | — |
| 2,048 | 1 | 2,047 | **571.891217** | 6.348949 | 571.891217 | 6.348949 |

The int8 carrier at ctx 2,048 is 515.801 (0.902× F32), the S1 row. The carrier comparison is
relative and unchanged. The pre-S1 run is the control for the S1 group read: the F32 path is
bit-identical at both the short and the long window, so S1 did not move the baseline.

## The 0.6B point is the low-rate end of the dial

`bench/cpu/1794822341-nq06-ppl-vs-q4.md`, same pack and byte-identical skeleton, ctx 512:

| arm | tokens | PPL | ratio to Q4_K_M |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Q4_K_M | 8,176 | 21.715296 | 1.000 |
| NQ v3 | 8,176 | 54.806671 | **2.524** |
| Q4_K_M | 2,044 | 18.118871 | 1.000 |
| NQ v3 | 2,044 | 74.296766 | **4.101** |

`nq06-v3.nqp` is the November 12 reference campaign's allocation (q/o rank 640, k/v 480,
gate/up/down 736 of a 1,024-wide hidden), 0.977 b/w. It is one setting of the same NanoQuant dial
that produced the 8B family: the rate is the rank allocation, and quality falls as the rate falls.
There is no "bad pack" and no separate approach; 74.3 PPL is what this model scores at 0.977 b/w
and ctx 512.

The layer-0 control of the same 0.977441 b/w step is 57.114113 at ctx 512
(`bench/cpu/1794834689-nq06-l0-1174.md`); the same receipt measures the column-RMS fallback at
1,225,029 PPL, which is why that fallback is now refused as a quality path.

## The 8B points are the same dial at higher rates

Cited, not re-run here; all rows use the same Q4_K_M teacher and raw wikitext-2 test:

| pack | b/w | ctx | scored | PPL | ratio to Q4_K_M | receipt |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 8B v3 | 3.130640 | 128 | 1,008 | 16.295301 | 1.011 | `1794653685` |
| 8B v2 | 1.124186 | 2,048 | 1,023 | 12.189530 | 1.607 | `1794879503` |
| 8B late2 | 1.178785 (dedup) | 2,048 | 1,023 | 11.374839 | 1.499 | `1794896874` |
| 8B targeted | 1.180 | 2,048 | 1,023 | 11.797864 | 1.555 | `1794893449` |

The 0.6B 0.977 point and the 8B 1.124/1.178/3.13 points are the same dial at four rates; the pack
header is the rate. The 8B's PPL **falls** with context on both arms
(Q4_K_M 16.125 at ctx 128 to 7.587 at ctx 2,048; the v3 pack 16.295 to 12.190). A longer window
adding context lowers both. The 0.6B at 0.977 b/w does the opposite: its PPL rises monotonically
with window length on the same tokens. The most compressed tensors are exactly the ones long-range
attention reads (k/v rank 480 of 1,024, one sign per weight); more keys accumulate more score
noise. That mechanism is `model`; the curve is `measured`.

## What this establishes

- The S1 PPL gate is a carrier gate: 515.801 against 571.891 on one fixed pack, 0.902×.
- The S1 commit did not change the F32 baseline: `c38b8c14` and `fd6ad4b0` return 74.296766 at
  ctx 512 and 571.891217 at ctx 2,048, with identical mean nll.
- The 0.6B at 0.977 b/w scores 74.297 at ctx 512, 111.067 at ctx 1,024 and 571.891 at ctx 2,048 on
  the same 2,044-token stream. The 8B at 1.124–3.131 b/w scores 1.011–1.607× Q4_K_M, the 1.18
  points at 1.499–1.555×. The dial is monotone in rate; the 0.6B's long-window degradation is the
  mechanism to fix at that setting.
- The 20 tok/s S1/S2 work is a **rate** result on the fixed 0.6B pack; it carries no quality claim.

## Scope

Measured on the two packs named, one host and one stream. No same-model ctx-2,048 Q4 control (the

arm; no 0-shot accuracy. No claim here is `proved`.
