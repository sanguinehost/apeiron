# nq: one column-code read per block of factor rows

Criterion: **T2** (turn latency), partial. Grades: `measured` for the phase times, walls and rates;
`tested` for the pooled/per-column bit equality and the mutation; `model` for the instruction and
byte bounds and the traffic the nesting removes. No 7B, no 20 tok/s, no decode claim.

Model: `skeleton-q8head.gguf` + `nq06-v3.nqp` (0.977441 b/w, 196 linears, 418,381,824 sign
terms/token from `1795505256`, 28 layers, 229,376 B/position KV), `--kernel q8act` (the harness
default, `examples/prefix_solve.rs:54`). Machine: i5-10210U (4 cores / 8 threads, 6 MB L3), 4
threads, `powersave`, every run under `~/Workspace/tools/agent-guard`. Times are the log clock,
AEDT (UTC+11).

## Result

The blocked Q8 reader is **1.09×** the per-row reader on the 1,827-position cold projection sum
(8 same-binary pairs, 95% CI [1.03, 1.16]) and **1.07×** on the cold wall (CI [1.01, 1.13]).
All 15 pairs, including the seven from the build before the `0`-arm fix, give **1.12**
[1.05, 1.19] on the phase sum and **1.09** [1.02, 1.15] on the wall, three pairs below 1. The
229-position window agrees at 1.09–1.14.

The absolute rate is a property of the window, not the kernel: block 4 measures **24.76 G terms/s**
in the first window and **18.14** in the last (different builds; the early one's pre-fix pooled
scratch sits outside the 32-term loop), so the 30 G/s target in `1795505256` is **1.21× / 1.65×
away** at 1,827 and met only in the early 229 window (33.60 G/s, 52.5 MB of KV per pass against
419.1 MB). The instruction bound computed below is 38–40 G terms/s, so 30 G/s is 0.75–0.78 of the
device ceiling; the measured kernel sits at 0.45–0.65 of that bound.

## What changed

`project_pooled` walks the factor rows of one stage in blocks of `R` and folds each block against
one read of the column's Q8 code and shift planes. Per 32-term block the two plane loads and the
constant setup serve `R` rows instead of one; the per-row word load, digit select, pair sum and
shift-add, and the per-row `mul_q32` and output-row sums, keep their order.

`APEIRON_NQ_BLOCK` is parsed once in a `OnceLock`: `0` and `1` take the per-row loop, `2..=8` the
block, absent or malformed takes `NQ_BLOCK = 4`, and a larger value clamps to `NQ_BLOCK_MAX = 8`.
The switch selects nesting only. The blocked path is Q8 only: `Reader::Exact` and `Reader::Ternary`
have no per-column plane to share, so they call the per-row entry, and a single-column call
(decode, and the last token of a prefill) never reaches the pooled loop at all
(`src/engine/step/cols.rs:508` requires `xs.len() > 1`).

| llama.cpp | what it does | adopted |
|---|---|---|
| `ggml-cpu.c:1202-1203`, `:1211-1251` | 16×16 outer tile, K loop inside the tile | the nesting: a factor-row block against one activation read, block loop innermost; `R = 4`, not 16 |
| `repack.cpp:1788`, `:1808-1812` | 4 accumulator rows × 8 columns, K inside | one vector accumulator per factor row, K inside |
| `arch/x86/repack.cpp:1113`, `:1192` | `__m256 acc_rows[16]`; LHS rows in groups of four | `R` accumulators; four rows keeps the five constants and the shift vectors off the spill path |
| `repack.cpp:2442` | 16×1 kernel | the narrow shape is the same loop with `R` rows |
| `repack.h:23-25` | 8-block-interleaved pack layout | **not** adopted: the pack is unchanged, so the plane is read at its stored stride |

## The device ceiling

Two bounds on the projection rate, and the measured anchor between them:

| bound | count | value | 30 G/s |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| instruction stream | 10 instructions per 32 terms per row (word load, broadcast, `vpsrlv`, two `vpshufb`, `vpand`, `vpmaddubsw`, `vpmaddwd`, `vpsllv`, `vpaddd`); the two plane loads are 2 per block of 4 rows | **40.2 G terms/s** at the measured 3.14 G instructions/s per core × 4 (equal-IPC model) | 0.75 |
| instruction stream, 10.5 (with the shared loads) | | 38.3 G/s | 0.78 |
| bytes | 51.12 MB/token (418,381,824 terms × 0.977441 b/w) | 183 G/s at the measured 22.33 GB/s; 349 G/s at the 42.656 GB/s spec peak (`1794919269`) | not binding |
| measured anchor | the same `code_select_avx2` reader on the 8B shapes, 12–14 instructions per 32 terms | 33.4 G/s (`1794919269`) | 0.90 |

The instruction stream is the binding bound. The 8B reader sits at its own count's bound (12
instructions give 33.5 G/s against 33.4 measured), which validates the count as the ceiling; the
0.6B transfer assumes the same per-core rate (equal IPC). The 0.6B blocked reader reaches 0.45–0.65
of its bound; the difference is the short linears' per-row horizontal fold and epilogue, spread over
15–32 words per row instead of 64–128 (`1795505256`). At 30 G/s the projection would take 13.9
ms/token against the bound's 10.4.

## What the nesting removes (`model`)

Per 32 terms the per-row reader is 12 instructions: two plane loads, the word load, the broadcast,
and eight vector operations. The block reader is the same per-row work with the two plane loads
amortised over `R` rows (2 per block, 0.5 per row at `R = 4`), 12 → 10.5 instructions per row, a
12.5% issue reduction. The weight words, the activation words and the output traffic are unchanged,
and the pack keeps its stored layout.

## What remains

- The effect is 1.09–1.12× on the phase sum, not the 2× a 15.3 → 30 G/s target implies. The
  remaining lever is the per-row epilogue and the per-column pack, not a wider block: block 8 does
  not separate from block 4 on the laptop, and the issue bound is 38–40 G/s.
- 30 G/s at 1,827 is **1.2–1.7× away** and the gap moves with machine state; a quiet-window run is
  needed before any rate is quoted as the device's.
- The 0.6B fixture only; the 7B pack, the Vulkan path and the 20 tok/s product claim are untouched.
- The tail token is attention-dominant, and decode never enters the blocked loop; neither is
  advanced here.
- The blocked body's twin is `tested`, not `proved`; the maintainer gate (`proof/regen.sh --check`,
  `cd proof/corpus && lake build`) has not run. No claim about rustc/LLVM code generation, the
  kernel ABI or the `unsafe` container.
