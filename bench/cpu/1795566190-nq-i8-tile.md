# The int8 carrier's prefill tile: `attn.core` 1.30×, cold wall 1.066×

Criterion: **T2** (turn latency), partial. Grades: `measured` for the box walls, rates, phase
splits and PPL; `tested` for the tile's twin/body equality and its per-column bound; `model` for
the laptop. No 7B, no decode claim, no laptop claim.

Model: `skeleton-q8head.gguf` + `nq06-v3.nqp` (0.977441 b/w, 196 linears, 418,381,824 sign
terms/token, 28 layers, 8 KV heads × 128, 229,376 B/position charged), `--kernel q8act` (the
harness default). Machine: OVH box `ubuntu@139.99.223.161` (Sydney; 8 vCPU EPYC Milan, 8 sockets ×
1 core × 1 thread, 31.3 GiB, 256 MiB L3), 8 participants, no GPU. Times UTC. The laptop, the only
device a timed claim may name, is **not measured**. Every run under `~/bin/box-run` (agent-guard,
16 GiB cap, 8 jobs); the example was rebuilt with `touch src/lib.rs` before the timed runs.

## Result

The int8 key/value carrier now reads its prefill attention through the same 64-position key tile as
the `f32` carrier. Box cold prefill at 1,827 tokens, 8 participants, rotating arms, three
alternating pairs:

| round | `attn.core` tile | per column | column/tile | cold wall tile | per column | column/tile |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 3,246.1 ms | 4,204.0 ms | 1.295 | 14.123 s | 14.902 s | 1.055 |
| 2 | 3,249.6 ms | 4,233.2 ms | 1.303 | 13.980 s | 15.154 s | 1.084 |
| 3 | 3,205.0 ms | 4,217.7 ms | 1.316 | 13.655 s | 14.812 s | 1.085 |
| paired mean | | | **1.305** | | | **1.075** |

The leaf is **1.299×** by medians (4,217.7 → 3,246.1 ms) and the cold wall **1.066×** (14.902 →
13.980 s). `attn.core` moves from 27.9–28.5% to 23.0–23.5% of the traced cold turn.

The tile does not make int8 the prefill carrier. The `f32` tile in the same window is **13.125 /
13.154 / 13.452 s** cold wall and **2,138.1 / 2,139.8 / 2,197.7 ms** `attn.core`, so the int8 tile
costs **1.063×** that wall and **1.517×** that leaf. The int8 carrier's purpose is decode (14.75
against 9.61 tok/s at 1,827, `1795177681-nq06-kv-i8.md`) and the KV byte budget; the tile closes most
of the prefill penalty the carrier used to pay (1.133× the `f32` tile wall per column, 1.063×
tiled), and the default stays `f32`.

|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| i8 | 1 | 521.755064 | 6.257198 | 95.8 | 455.4 MiB |
| i8 | 0 | 515.800858 | 6.245721 | 91.5 | 456.5 MiB |
| f32 | 1 | 572.461723 | 6.349946 | 97.9 | 789.5 MiB |

The int8 tile's PPL is **1.0115×** the int8 per-column path. The `f32` tile's is 572.462 against the
572.461-adjacent `f32` per-column figure of 571.89 in `1795177681-nq06-kv-i8.md` (+0.10%, a
cross-run comparison on the same stream), so the tiled recurrence itself is not what moves the
int8 number: the int8 tile's score dot accumulates `head_dim` terms sequentially where the
per-column core sums eight independent lanes, and on quantised rows that dot-order difference is
worth about one percent of PPL. The carrier gate in `1795177681` (within 1.01× `f32`) was measured
against the per-column read; the tile is inside 1.02× of it, which this receipt states rather than
assumes. The per-position argmax dump was not reduced to a top-1 agreement here.

## Why the words change only within the bound

The tiled recurrence is `1795560104-attn-tile-gemm.md`'s: one row maximum and one accumulator
rescale per 64-position key tile instead of one per position, with the same score FMA order and the
same value accumulation order. The int8 arm adds the carrier's own product order: the key scale
multiplies the score after the dot (`dot · (ks[row] · scale)`) and the value scale multiplies the
probability before the value dot (`(w · vs[row]) · code`), which is the per-column int8 group
core's order. The codes are exact in binary32, so the pack from code to `f32` is lossless.

Three checks hold:

- **Twin/body.** `attn_core_i8_tile` against `attn_core_i8_tile_scalar` is word-equal over the
  fixture's head widths, strides, group sizes, tile edges and scale patterns
  (`test_attn_core_i8_query_tile_agrees_with_the_per_query_core`), as the `f32` arm is.
- **Tile against the per-column core.** The two evaluate the same real recurrence with a different
  score-dot order, so the test asserts a 2e-3 relative bound; the fixture's largest observed
  relative gap is 1.26e-3. The step's own
  `the_i8_tile_group_read_agrees_with_the_per_column_read` asserts the Q32.32 tile bound
  `64 + (m·512·(prefix + 64) >> 32)` plus `m/500` for the dot-order term, over first positions
  that cross the 64-position tile boundary.
- **Model level.** The PPL row above: +1.15% against the same carrier's per-column read.

## Ceiling

Under the per-column int8 read (the unmodified carrier path) `attn.core` is 28.2–28.5% of the cold
traced turn, so the phase can move the cold wall by at most `1/(1 − 0.282) = 1.393×` however fast
its kernel becomes. The measured wall ratio is 1.066×, which is 17% of the excess the ceiling admits
(`0.066 / 0.393`), and the leaf ratio is 1.305×. The projection phases (`ffn` + `attn.proj_in` +
`attn.proj_out`, 66.6% of the cold wall in the `f32` split) remain the dominant term at this
length; this change is the matching prefill read for the carrier that owns the byte budget, not the
dominant-term fix.

The K/V stream the leaf moves falls from 12.55 GB to 3.23 GB per cold turn (54,723 rows/(head,layer)
× 1 KiB × 224 for `f32`; × 1/4 for the code plane plus the scale plane for `i8`). The leaf does
**not** get 3.9× faster, so at this length the tile is conversion-bound, not byte-bound: each tile
pass converts `2 · 64 · 128` codes to binary32 before the GEMM. That conversion is the open cost.

## Omissions

- The laptop timing is not measured. The box is Zen 2 with its own integer-vector throughput; the
  gain is a `model` for the laptop until the laptop run.
- The maintainer gate (`proof/regen.sh --check`, `lake build`) does not run on either host. The tile
  renamed the AVX2 body to `attn_core_tile_avx2_inner` and moved the `f32`/`i8` row source into one
  body over `TileRows`; the opaque entry in `proof/regen.sh` and the twin list in `proof/README.md`
  are updated, but neither is checked against the pinned Aeneas and Charon.
- The GPU/SPIR-V tier is not run (no device, no `glslc` pin).
- Decode is untouched: the tile needs `k > 1`. The int8 carrier's decode rate and the decode-time
  `attn.core` share are `1795177681`'s and the context work's.
- The 8B is not measured; its `group`, `n_head_kv` and head width change the tile's row count and
  the conversion volume, and the microkernel handles any row count and any `head_dim % 8 == 0`.
