# The U and V sign planes: 97.6% of the 8B pack, and 91.5 MB of it is the qkv split

Criterion **Q1**, with Q2 context. Grade: `measured` for every byte count, word count, hash,
density and the 1,188 byte-identity checks below; `model` for the ADMM mapping, the instruction
counts, and the rank-marginal arithmetic. The result is a rate finding, not a quality fix: the
pack is **1.124186 b/w**, while the source's NQ tensor payload is **1.018855 b/w** at identical
factors. The difference is the converter writing the fused qkv `V` (and `s_pre`, `s_mid`, the
indices) three times. U and V are pure signs; the kernel never approximates them, and the 1.607×
PPL gap is the fit, not these bits.

## What U and V are

A fitted linear is

```text
Ŵ_ij = s_post,i · s_pre,j · Σ_k s_mid,k · U_ik · V_kj + S_ij,
U ∈ {±1}^{out×rank},  V ∈ {±1}^{rank×inn},  S[o, idx[c]] = salient[o, c].
```

The same object as `rank` rank-1 sign outer products:

```text
Ŵ = Σ_k s_mid,k · (s_post ⊙ u_k) (s_pre ⊙ v_k)^T + S,
```

where `u_k` is column `k` of `U` and `v_k` is row `k` of `V`. `U` is the **output-side** factor:
`out` rows, each a sign pattern over the `rank` features. `V` is the **input-side** factor: `rank`
rows, each a sign pattern over the `inn` inputs. `s_mid[k]` is the one continuous weight of rank
component `k`; `s_pre` and `s_post` are the input and output diagonal scalings. The signs are not
the sign of `W`: `Ŵ_ij` is a sum of `rank` signed `s_mid` values, not a stored bit.

The pipeline is the three stages the kernel runs:

```text
x (inn)
  │ diag(s_pre)
  ▼
t (inn)
  │ V: rank rows, each Σ_j V_kj·t_j
  ▼
Vt (rank)
  │ diag(s_mid)
  ▼
z (rank)
  │ U: out rows, each Σ_k U_ik·z_k
  ▼
Uz (out)
  │ diag(s_post), + S·x[idx]
  ▼
y (out)
```

The source fit is the upstream `SamsungLabs/NanoQuant` at `a9e0a43`: `admm_nq.py` normalizes `W`
by the input and output norms, factorizes the normalized matrix with ADMM, projects the two
latents to signs, and takes `s_pre`/`s_post` as mean absolute values; the export carries the
per-rank `s_mid` the engine applies between the factors. `quality.json` names
`factorization.shared_input` with the group `self_attn.attn_qkv`, which is why the source qkv has
one `V`. This mapping is a pointer to the upstream code and config, not a re-run.

## Layout, words and bits

A v2 section is 32 B: `layer, kind, out, inn, rank, u_words, v_words, k`. Then

- `U`: row-major `[out, u_words]` little-endian `u32`, `u_words = ⌈rank/32⌉`.
- `V`: row-major `[rank, v_words]` little-endian `u32`, `v_words = ⌈inn/32⌉`; this converter takes
  `inn` from `v_words · 32`, so V has no padding in this pack.
- `s_pre` (`inn`), `s_mid` (`rank`), `s_post` (`out`) bf16 words; `salient_idx` (`k`) `u32`;
  `salient` (`out × k`) f16 words.

Bit `b` of word `w` is column `32w + b`; bit 1 is `+1`, bit 0 is `-1`. Every one of the 252 ranks
is a multiple of 32 (minimum 1824, maximum 4096), so there is no padding: the stored U bits equal
`out·rank` exactly, and the stored V bits equal `rank·inn` exactly. A future rank that is not a
multiple of 32 would pay up to 31 padding bits per U row.

For every kind the measured U+V rate is exactly

```text
b/w(U+V) = rank/inn + rank/out,
```

to six decimals, because U costs `out·rank` bits over `out·inn` weights and V costs `rank·inn`
bits over the same weights. The U/V byte split is `out/(out+inn)`: a square linear is 50/50, a
1024×4096 k/v is 20/80, a 12288×4096 gate is 75/25, and a 4096×12288 down is 25/75. Rank cancels
from the split; it sets the total. The low-rank form is not a compression of a dense sign matrix:
for q, `rank/inn + rank/out = 1.199`, more than one dense sign bit per weight; the model class is
a rank-limited sum of signs, not a per-weight sign.

The sign planes are **952,877,056 B**, **97.63%** of the pack. Their bit densities are
**0.497790** (U) and **0.498038** (V): near-balanced, maximum-entropy signs, so there is no
run-length or entropy-coding win. The only rate levers are rank, sharing, or a different model.

A concrete word: layer 0 q, `U` row 0 word 0 is `0xd4abdf75`; bit 0 first, its 32 signs are

```text
+-+-+++- +++++-++ ++-+-+-+ --+-+-++
```

`V` row 0 word 0 is `0x0f40a313`:

```text
++--+--- ++---+-+ ------+- ++++----
```

The kernel's `sign_dot!` maps bit 1 to `-t` (`(t ^ mask) - mask` with `mask = -bit`), which is the
negative of the file's `+1`. `U` and `V` are always read as a product, so the two negations cancel;
a residual plane is read once and its dot is explicitly negated. Anyone using `U` or `V` alone
must account for that inversion.

## The qkv split: 91,450,016 B of duplication

The source has one fused `blk.L.attn_qkv` factor: `U` is `6144 × rank`, `V` is `rank × 4096`,
and `s_pre`, `s_mid`, `salient_idx` are one copy. `tools/nq_convert.py` splits it by output row
into q (0..4096), k (4096..5120) and v (5120..6144):

- `U` is row-sliced, so the three U payloads are disjoint row blocks. `post` and `salient` are
  row-sliced too.
- `V`, `s_pre`, `s_mid` and `salient_idx` are written whole in every section.

That is exact for the fused factor, and it is byte-identical: 1,188 payload checks over 36 layers
and 252 sections, 0 mismatches. For layer 0 and layer 35 the q/k/v `V` sha256 values are identical,
and layer 0's is the source's fused `V`:

```text
layer 0  V_q = V_k = V_v = 9497d1392a999830cf55149162d16d5270a28ed5a4bbe2f6bb7259dadfca64e7
layer 35 V_q = V_k = V_v = cc62a8751e7879702f3f394feda16fe85bf570ff018e24506108495f3f61c33c
```

The fused qkv block is `113,131,520 B` of U+V, **0.998987 b/w** over 905,969,664 weights. The
split block is `203,636,736 B`, **1.798177 b/w**. The duplicate V alone is **90,505,216 B**; with
the duplicated `s_pre`, `s_mid` and `salient_idx` the duplicate is **91,450,016 B**, **9.369%** of
the factor payload. Deduping V only gives 885,536,368 B (885,544,464 B with the header,
**1.019953 b/w**); deduping all four shared payloads gives the source's own **884,591,568 B**
(884,599,664 B with the header, **1.018865 b/w**). The upstream config already requests this
sharing; the loss is in the converter, not the fit.

## Execution: one sign-dot core, two stages

`NqLinear` keeps `u` and `v` as `Plane<u32>`: on Linux they are read-only slices of the pack's
`mmap`, not copied or unpacked. `from_packed` decodes the bf16 scales to Q32.32 once; `project_with`
builds `t = s_pre ⊙ x`, then runs two row loops:

1. **V stage.** For each of `rank` rows: `dot = Σ_j V_kj·t_j`; `z_k = mul_q32(dot, s_mid[k])`.
   `V` rows are 128 words for every kind except `down`, which is 384 words. `t` is quantized once
   per projection under `--kernel q8act`; the exact path uses the Q32.32 words.
2. **U stage.** For each of `out` rows: `acc = Σ_k U_ik·z_k`; `low = acc · s_post[i]`; add the
   sparse `corr` and the residual, then round once. `U` rows are 76–83 words for q/k/v, 63–65 for
   o, 84–117 for gate, 57–102 for up, 95–128 for down. `z` is quantized once per projection under
   q8act.

Both stages call the same `sign_dot` core. The q8 loop is eleven vector instructions per 32 terms:
five selector instructions (`vpbroadcastd`, `vpsrlvd`, `vpshufb`, `vpand`, `vpshufb`), then
`vmovdqu` codes, `vpmaddubsw`, `vpmaddwd`, `vmovdqu` shift, `vpsllvd`, `vpaddd`. The exact
four-plane loop is twenty-one: the same five selector instructions plus four planes of
`vmovdqu`, `vpmaddubsw`, `vpmaddwd`, `vpaddd`. Every U and V length here is a multiple of 32, so
the scalar tail is unreachable; the whole dot is the vector loop. The sign dots are exact integer
sums in both readers; the only rounding is `mul_q32`: one per pre-scaled activation, one per rank,
and one on the output sum.

Per token the pack reads **123,469,824 U words** (493,879,296 B) and **114,749,440 V words**
(458,997,760 B): 238,219,264 words, **7,623,016,448 sign terms**, U 51.83% and V 48.17%. With the
Q8_0 head the token reads 1,637,275,152 B; at the measured 2.25 tok/s that is 3.68 GB/s, of which
U is 1.11 GB/s and V 1.03 GB/s. The duplicate qkv V is 724,041,728 terms per token, 9.5% of the
sign work; sharing it would remove that work as well as the 90.5 MB.

## What this means for the 6.6%

The exact-vs-q8act A/B holds U and V fixed and changes only the activation representation:
12.189530 vs 12.187527 at ctx 2048, 14.879110 vs 14.903740 at ctx 512. The reader is at most 0.17%
and sign-inconsistent, so the gap is \(W - \widehat W\), the rank/sign fit, not the stored signs
and not the sign dot.

The 1.18 band's headroom is **48,459,111 B**. One extra rank across all 252 sections costs
`Σ(out+inn)/8 + 2·252 = 341,496 B` (the sign words plus one `s_mid` word per section), so the
headroom buys **141.9 rank steps**, **+0.563 rank per section**, **0.021%** of the 2648.5 mean.
After deduping all shared payloads the headroom is **139,909,127 B**, **+1.626 rank per section**,
**0.061%**. Spending it all on `down` buys +52.7 rank per down section (1.66%). None of these is a
6.6% PPL lever. The r2 residual, which does reach 1.148× at ctx 128, adds **1,742,046,192 B** at
+2.006 b/w; the 1.18 headroom is 2.8% of it, and the deduped 139.9 MB is 8.0%. But the deduped
headroom, **0.161 b/w**, buys 8.0% of the weights at two bits. The two-plane residual is worth 0.2041
nats/bw at ctx 2048 — the full 3.130640 b/w pack scores 8.094085, 1.067×, against the base's
12.189530 — so the 1.5× bar needs 0.3364 b/w and the cap's 0.161 is **47.8%** of it (`1794893449`).
A uniform 8% spend is modelled at 1.554981; the 19-section salient-mass selection measures 1.598041,
0.164× of uniform per byte. The quality work is not a residual at 1.18 b/w; it is a selection or a
fit that uses the same bytes 2.1× better, and that is the open question.

The rate work is therefore the qkv sharing (a bit-exact 0.105 b/w) plus the 1.8-rank trim it makes
affordable. The quality work is a **selection or a fit at 1.18 b/w**, not more bytes: the cap is 48% of the
0.3364 b/w the bar needs (`1794893449`).

## What this does not cover

- No new PPL, no 0-shot, no 1.15–1.18 b/w pack, no 7B point.
- The qkv sharing is not implemented; the 1.018865 b/w and the 9.5% term saving are arithmetic on
  the measured payloads, not a serving run.
- The instruction counts are static; the A/B and T1 receipts carry the measured performance.
- The pack is v2 (no residual); the r2 numbers are from the prior receipt.
- The ADMM mapping describes the upstream fit; it is not a claim that `nq_fit` reproduces it.
