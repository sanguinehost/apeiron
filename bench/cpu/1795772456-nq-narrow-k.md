# Narrow-k is the decode bottleneck; the row-band fix is refused

Criterion: **T1** decode rate. Grade: `measured` on the OVH box; the ratios below are ratios, not
claims. The laptop pair is still blocked (`MemAvailable` ~0.9 GiB against the 2 GiB guard), so this
is CPU-tier box evidence only.

## Result

Decode's projection is limited by **how few of the eight participants a verify pass can fill**, and
by a per-column cost that is 3.1× the prefill column cost. A row-banded narrow path that fills all
eight participants and reads each factor row once was built, was **exact** on the decode stream,
and was **18% slower**: 6.78–6.96 ms/token against 5.79. It is refused; the tree is unchanged.

## The k-curve

`prefill_width` at 8 threads, ms/token, mean of 3 (`--tokens k --reps 3`), box `nq06-v3.nqp`:

| k | 1 | 2 | 4 | 8 | 9 | 16 | 32 | 64 | 256 | 1827 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| ms/token | 10.10 | 10.50 | 5.63 | 3.22 | 4.58 | 2.90 | 2.94 | 1.84 | 1.84 | 1.94 |

Three facts:

1. **k=1 and k=2 cost the same wall.** `band = ceil(k/width)` gives one column per task, so k=1
   runs on one participant and k=2 on two; the wall is flat because the work is spread, not
   reduced.
2. **k=9 is 1.4× k=8.** The extra column is the leftover loop (the 8-wide tile is not used), and
   draft 8's verify pass is exactly k=9.
3. **Per column, decode costs 3.1× prefill**: 3.56 ms/token at k≈4.6 against 1.13 ms/token at
   k=1,827. Both move the same 0.418 GMAC/column (`nq_stats`), so the difference is the tile: at
   k≥8 a band folds eight columns per weight read; below it the leftover loop folds one or two.

Thread scaling is clean, so the box is not bandwidth-capped: k=64 at 1/2/4/8 threads is
1,140 / 622 / 205 / 116 ms (9.8× for 8 threads). At k=8 it is 126 / 77 / 41 / 26 ms (4.9×).

## The refused change

`band = ceil(k/width)` splits columns; when k < 8 it leaves participants idle and reads the whole
factor plane once per column. The change gave each task a **band of output rows and all k columns**
(`for_pbands`, V then U), so every participant is filled and each factor row is read once per
call. It is bit-exact: `lookup_accept` reported `exact`, 102/102, and `engine::kern` passed 87
tests. It is slower:

| arm | wall, 7 runs | attn.proj_in | attn.proj_out | ffn | head | pool.wait |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| column bands (shipped) | 5.79 ms/token | 0.921 | 0.446 | 2.244 | 1.199 | 0.444 |
| row bands | 6.78–6.96 ms/token | 1.348 | 0.627 | 2.338 | 1.261 | 0.514 |

The mechanism is in the trace: `proj_in` +46% and `proj_out` +41%, `ffn` only +4%. The row path
builds `t` (and the `z` readers) on the dispatching thread, so `k·inn` `mul_q32` calls that the
column path ran on `k` participants in parallel run serially; that is 4.7k–14k serial multiplies
per linear, which is a large share of a projection whose fold is only 5/8 under-threaded. The
saving it buys is bounded by `8/k` on the fold and the fold is not the whole projection.

A corrected form (column bands for `t`+`V`, row bands for `U` only) would gain at most
`1/(0.55 + 0.45·k/8)` ≈ 1.23× at k≈4.6, and cannot reach the 1.55× the decode target needs
(5.79 → 3.57 ms/token). It is not built.

## Prefill, for the same reason

`prefill_width --tokens 1827 --reps 1` with `APEIRON_TRACE=1` (the trace print is the one addition
this receipt commits), 3,594 ms:

| phase | ms | share |
|---|---:|---:|
| attn.core | 1,288 | 35.8% |
| ffn | 1,243 | 34.6% |
| attn.proj_in | 542 | 15.1% |
| attn.proj_out | 273 | 7.6% |
| norm | 143 | 4.0% |
| residual | 80 | 2.2% |
| embed | 23 | 0.6% |
| pool.wait (overlay) | 194 | 5.4% |

The projection tile is already at the AVX2 floor: `code_select_row_c_avx2` is 28.2% of cycles and
covers the 764e9 sign MACs at **25.8 MACs/cycle**, against a 25 MACs/cycle 8-column floor
(`4 uops/cycle ÷ 0.156 uops/MAC`). The other half of the projection time is per-row machinery, and
attention is at 2.5 MACs/cycle overall (its score/value GEMM is at 13.5, i.e. 84% of the 16
MACs/cycle float-FMA floor; the rest is tile packing and the online softmax).

Prefill 0.5× is 2,080 ms, so it needs 1.7×. Both phases need the per-row machinery cut, and prefill
additionally needs attention's non-GEMM half; neither is a narrow-k problem.

## Scope

- Box only, no laptop row.
- The row-band experiment was reverted, not tuned; the refusal is of the shape as built. Its
  exactness result (`lookup_accept` `exact`, 87 `engine::kern` tests) stands and is why the
  mechanism, not correctness, is the reason for refusal.
- The k-curve is `prefill_width`, whose per-token cost includes the full layer pass and excludes
  the head; it is a shape measurement, not a decode wall.
- Rust/LLVM codegen, the kernel ABI and the `unsafe` container are outside the claim.
