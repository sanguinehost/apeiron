# Qwen3-8B at 1.124 b/w: 1.607× Q4_K_M at ctx 2048, 2.25 tok/s on the laptop

Criterion: **Q2** for the quality and the **Q1** rate. Grade: `measured` for every PPL and tok/s
below; the ratios and the byte fractions are arithmetic and `model`. The rate–quality corner is
**not met**: at 1.124186 b/w the pack misses the 1.5× bar by **1.07×**, and the laptop decode
misses the 20 tok/s working target by **8.9×**.

PPL host: vast.ai instance `60976732`, AMD EPYC 7402 (24 cores, 12 effective, 48 threads visible),
125 GiB RAM, Ubuntu 24.04; the RTX 5080 is unused by these runs. Laptop host: Intel Core i5-10210U
(4C/8T), 6.7 GiB, Ubuntu.

## Result

The upstream `arelath/Qwen3-8B-nanoquant-GGUF` image, converted by `tools/nq_convert.py` with no
dense reference and no residual plane, stores 6,945,767,424 fitted weights at **1.124186 b/w** in
976,049,680 B. With the 1,329,625,696 B skeleton the pair is 2,305,675,376 B, **45.86%** of the
5,027,783,488 B Q4_K_M file. On the same token stream at ctx 2048 it scores **12.189530** against
**7.587145**, a ratio of **1.606603**. That ratio is against the stock file, not on the same head:
the NQ skeleton's `output.weight` is `Q8_0` (8.5 bits/weight) and the stock file's is `Q6_K` (6.56).
The more precise head is on the NQ arm, so the ratio is biased toward NQ and is not a same-head
result.

Q4_K_M peak RSS is 4795.8 MiB at ctx 512 and 5275.4 MiB at ctx 2048. The 1.5× bar is 11.380718 at

The ctx-128 row of `1794553293-nq8b-q4km-ppl.md` compares Qwen3-8B-Base images against the
Qwen3-8B instruct source and is not a bar. The same-checkpoint ctx-128 pair is 28.307562 against
16.124694 (1.7555, `1794653685-nq8b-residual-ppl.md`); from ctx 128 to 2048 the ratio moves
1.7555 → 1.6066 and is then flat. The 3.130640 b/w residual pack scores 16.295301 against the same
16.124694 at ctx 128 (1.011×). The rate–quality curve now has three product-context points:
1.124 b/w → 1.607, 1.284 b/w → 1.598 (a 19-section two-plane splice of the r2 pack, 1.178909 b/w
after the qkv duplicate), and 3.131 b/w → **1.067** (8.094085, measured at ctx 2048); receipt

ctx-128 value, so the 1.5× bar needs 0.3364 b/w; the 1.18 cap's 0.161 b/w is 47.8% of it.

## Laptop

The pack and skeleton load in `examples/generate` on the laptop, both files mapped:

```
APEIRON_THREADS=4 systemd-run --user --scope -p MemoryMax=2000M -p MemorySwapMax=0 -- \
  target/release/examples/generate --model skeleton.gguf --pack model.nqp \
  --tokenizer tokenizer.json --chat --prompt "What is 17 times 23?" --n 2 --kernel q8act
prefill  6.225 s, 4.66 tok/s
decode   0.888 s, 2.25 tok/s (2 tokens, stop: length)
```

2.25 tok/s is 8.9× short of the 20 tok/s working target. The token reads 1,637,275,152 B: the 976,049,680 B pack plus the Q8_0 `output.weight` at 661,225,472 B; at 2.25 tok/s that is 3.68 GB/s against the laptop's measured bandwidth, so bytes are not binding. The 6,945,767,424 sign terms per token are: at the measured 0.75 instructions per term the projection stream is 5.21e9 instructions per token, and 2.25 tok/s is 11.7e9 instructions/s on four cores. T1's arithmetic puts 20 tok/s at 0.194 instructions per term or at ≤2.13e9 terms per token, neither of which this pack provides. The 2,305,675,376 B pair runs under a 2.0 GiB cgroup because the two files are mapped and the resident set is the touched pages; the 4.0 GB residual pair needed a 4.6 GiB scope on the same laptop.

## Protocol

```
examples/ppl --model <gguf> [--pack model.nqp] --tokenizer tokenizer.json \
  --data wiki.test.raw --tokens 2048 --skip 0 --ctx <512|2048> --head-batch 16 \
  --kernel q8act --second-half
```

`--second-half` scores positions `ctx/2 .. ctx-2` of each window, the span llama.cpp's perplexity
uses: 4 windows × 255 positions at ctx 512 and 1 window × 1,023 at ctx 2048. Tokens are the first
2,048 of raw wikitext-2 test, `--skip 0`.

## What this does not cover

- One dataset (raw wikitext-2 test), one tokenizer, 2,048 tokens, one window at ctx 2048. The
  earlier ctx-512 run of the same pack reported 14.903005 on the same 1,020 positions where this
  binary reports 14.879110; the two PPLs below are from one binary and one stream, and the 0.16%
  shift across harness revisions is not resolved.
- 0-shot accuracy, the other half of Q2, is not measured.
- The ratio is not a same-head comparison: the NQ `Q8_0` head is more precise than the stock
  `Q6_K` head, so the same-head ratio is not measured.
- The laptop tok/s is two greedy tokens of one prompt, not a mean over three runs at a 2k context.
- No ≥8,192-token row and no 200k context: C2 is not addressed.
- The pack is the upstream GPU pipeline's output, not `nq_fit`'s. Nothing here validates
  `learn::nanoquant`.
- The kernel is Rust and the extraction and Lean refinement of `src/engine/kern/nq.rs` are not run
  here (no pinned Aeneas/Charon toolchain). No claim in this receipt is `proved`.

## The 7B fit, unchanged

The Qwen2.5-7B T=200 campaign still writes no quality-bearing pack. The reference-ridge fit stops
at `gate`, iteration 22, B side, r = 3296, with `Solve(NotPositiveDefinite)` from the host
`spd_inverse`. The refused `hw` is positive definite in float64 (least eigenvalue 0.224, largest
1,686); the refusal is the Q32.32 Schur recursion, whose 824×824 base inverse has residual
7.76e-4 where Q32.32 allows `eps·κ·‖A‖ ≈ 2.3e-10 × 1.3e3 × 416 ≈ 1.2e-4`, and whose Schur
complement then loses 0.665 absolute against its float64 value. A solve-based (Cholesky)
complement or a wider word grid is the fix; a tolerance change is not. On the fractional RTX 5080
the T=200 layer-0 fit is 455 s for six kinds and `down` aborts with `vkAllocateMemory failed:
VkResult -2`, so 28 layers are hours of device time and the fractional slice cannot hold `down`.
