# The 0.6B at 1,827: int8 KV takes `attn.core` from 57.4% to 32.9%, 14.75 tok/s

Criterion: **T1** (decode) and **C1** (context state). Grade: `measured` for the rates, the split,
the PPL and the RSS; `tested` for the codec, the group read and the AVX2 equality; `model` for the
clean-host projection and the 1M byte budgets. Machine: ThinkPad L13 Yoga 20R6, i5-10210U (4 cores /
8 threads, 15 W, AVX2/BMI2/POPCNT, no AVX-512, no AVX-VNNI), 6.6 GiB, Ubuntu 24.04, `performance`,
`APEIRON_THREADS=4`. The machine was **contested**: a `node server.js` (SillyTavern, PID 3325647)
held CPU 6 at ~90%, load average was 1.1–2.2 and 4.2 GB of swap was in use. The rate runs are
pinned to CPUs 0,1,3,4. The clean F32 baseline is `1795170568`.

Result: the int8 KV carrier takes decode at 1,827 tokens from **9.61 to 14.75 tok/s** (three runs
each: 9.35–10.11 against 14.32–15.10), and `attn.core` from **58.71 to 21.76 ms/token**. The token
is 67.8 ms at the rate mean and 66.08 ms in the traced split; the dominant term is still
`attn.core` at 32.9% (ceiling 1.49×), and the Q8_0 head is second at 18.70 ms (28.3%). At 2,047
teacher-forced positions the carrier scores **515.80 PPL against F32's 571.89 (0.902×)** with
**79.1% top-1 agreement**, and peak RSS falls **783.5 → 450.9 MiB**. The 20 tok/s gate is **not
met** on the measured path: the head removal in S2 leaves 49.1 ms from the rate mean (20.4 tok/s)
before its own pack read, so S2 lands at 19.6–20.4 tok/s under this contention, not clear of 20.

## The rates

Pinned to CPUs 0,1,3,4, three runs per cell, arms interleaved:

| prefix | carrier | prefill tok/s (mean; runs) | decode tok/s (mean; runs) | wall ms/token |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 5 | F32 | 13.65 (15.01, 13.06, 12.89) | 21.49 (22.27, 21.46, 20.73) | 46.5 |
| 5 | I8 | 12.87 (12.96, 12.87, 12.79) | 20.98 (20.59, 21.34, 21.02) | 47.7 |
| 1,827 | F32 | 20.30 (19.45, 20.00, 21.46) | 9.61 (9.36, 9.35, 10.11) | 104.1 |
| 1,827 | I8 | 29.28 (28.74, 28.68, 30.42) | 14.75 (14.82, 15.10, 14.32) | 67.8 |

At prefix 5 the int8 append (448 rows quantised per token: 28 layers × 8 KV heads × K and V)
costs 2.4% on the mean; the read is too
short to pay it back. At 1,827 the read dominates and the carrier is **1.54×** on the decode mean
and 1.44× on prefill. The traced split puts the steady token at 66.08 ms against the 67.8 ms rate
mean, so the trace is inside the run's spread. The host is ~1.4–1.8× slower than the clean F32
baseline depending on the phase (`1795170568`), so the absolute rates are not comparable across
receipts; the within-session F32/I8 pair is.

## The split

`phase_split --prefix 1827 --steps 16`, same pinned set:

| phase | F32, ms/token | share | I8, ms/token | share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **attn.core** | **58.71** | **57.4%** | **21.76** | **32.9%** |
| head (Q8_0) | 18.56 | 18.1% | 18.70 | 28.3% |
| ffn | 14.17 | 13.8% | 14.29 | 21.6% |
| attn.proj_in | 6.75 | 6.6% | 7.09 | 10.7% |
| attn.proj_out | 3.18 | 3.1% | 3.19 | 4.8% |
| norm | 0.529 | 0.5% | 0.626 | 0.9% |
| residual | 0.217 | 0.2% | 0.228 | 0.3% |
| untraced | 0.225 | 0.2% | 0.192 | 0.3% |
| **token** | **102.34** | | **66.08** | |
| `pool.wait` (overlay) | 30.47 | 29.8% | 0.149 | 0.2% |

The F32 target owned 57.4% (ceiling 1.75×), so the KV read could move the token; the measured
2.70× on that phase is inside the ceiling. It is not one kernel ratio: `pool.wait` was 30.47 ms of
the F32 58.71, so the F32 phase's own work is 28.24 ms and the int8 work is 21.61 ms, a 1.31×
kernel reduction. The rest of the phase gain is the task count: one pool task per KV head instead
of one per query head halves the shared tasks (448 → 224 per token) and removes the wait. The int8
read issues 59,136 bytes per position against F32's 229,376, so its 21.61 ms is at **5.0 GB/s**
effective, not the 22 GB/s the previous receipt projected from a bandwidth-only model; the decode
and per-slot folding are the difference, and the int8 projection in `1795170568` is superseded.

`20 tok/s` is 50.0 ms. At the traced 66.08 ms the 18.70 ms head is 28.3% of the token; removing it
leaves 47.38 ms = 21.1 tok/s before the head's pack read, and a 1.5–2.0 ms pack head leaves
48.9–49.4 ms = 20.3–20.4. At the 67.8 ms rate mean the same arithmetic leaves 49.1 ms = 20.4
before the head and 50.6–51.1 ms = 19.6–19.8 after it. S2 is therefore marginal against 20 on this
host; the ffn (14.29 ms, 21.6%) is the next term if it falls short.

## The bytes

| term | F32 | I8 |
|---|---:|---:|
| per position, 28 layers × 8 KV heads × 128 × (K,V) | 229,376 B | 59,136 B |
| at 1,827 positions | 419.07 MB | 108.04 MB |
| at 2,048 positions (the PPL cap) | 469.76 MB | 121.11 MB |
| at 1M positions | 240.5 GB | 62.0 GB |

The int8 row is `2 · 128 + 2 · 4 = 264` bytes per KV head per position: 128 code bytes for K, 128
for V and two binary32 scales. The measured RSS delta at ctx 2,048 is 332.6 MiB against the
predicted 348.7 MB of plane (332.5 MiB): the carrier and nothing else explains the fall.

## The quality

`ppl --kernel q8act`, the same pack and token stream, one window of 511 or 2,047 scored positions:

| ctx | carrier | PPL | mean NLL | top-1 vs F32 | peak RSS |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 512 | Q32 | 347.683 | 5.851291 | 485/511 (94.9%) | 530.1 MiB |
| 512 | F32 | 346.316 | 5.847352 | — | 418.0 MiB |
| 512 | I8 | 320.319 | 5.769317 | 390/511 (76.3%) | 334.9 MiB |
| 2,048 | F32 | 571.891 | 6.348949 | — | 783.5 MiB |
| 2,048 | I8 | 515.801 | 6.245721 | 1,620/2,047 (79.1%) | 450.9 MiB |

F32 is within 0.4% of Q32's PPL at 512 and agrees with it on 94.9% of positions, so the two exact
carriers are the same model at this length. I8's PPL is 7.5% lower at 512 and 9.8% lower at 2k. The
top-1 agreement is 76–79%, so the carrier does move the ranking on weakly separated positions;
the PPL, which scores the whole distribution, is lower, and the receipt does not measure the logit
margins. The gate is "within 1.01× F32": pass. The absolute PPL is the 0.6B pack's (0.977441 b/w),
not a product claim; the carrier is the only variable between rows. The Q32 arm at ctx 2,048 was
started and stopped after five minutes in the prefill to free the host for the rate runs; the 512
row stands, and the 2k comparison is F32 against I8 only.

## Scope

- One model pair, one machine, one power profile, 4 threads, contested host. The clean-host
  three-run mean is not taken; the F32 arm above is the same-host control.
- The prefix is 1,827 copies of token 1; the split is timing, not quality.
- The default carrier stays F32; `APEIRON_KV_CARRIER=i8` is opt-in. The block certificate stays
  Q32-only, and `with_file` (cold tier) is Q32/F32 only.
- No selection, no cold tier, no 1M run. Dense 1M is still 62.0 GB int8, 240.5 GB F32.
- The maintainer gate is unrun on this host (no pinned Aeneas/Charon/Lean), and the extraction
  unit is stale for the NanoQuant landing before this change: `cargo check` on
  `proof/extract/Cargo.toml` fails on `crate::format::nq` and `crate::engine::TensorKey`, which the
  mirror does not carry (`proof/extract/lib.rs` predates `443f4305`). The i8 wiring is therefore
  in place but unexercised: the module is under the included `src/engine/attn.rs`, the AVX2 body is
  the new boundary axiom `attn.i8.attn_core_i8_group_avx2_inner`, and the committed
  `proof/ApeironWord.lean` still names `mixer.Carrier` for `KvState.carrier`. No i8 body has
  `proved` coverage; the engine refinement is open, as before.
