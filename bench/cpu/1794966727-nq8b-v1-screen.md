## Against OG

`1.525440 / 1.78 = 0.8570`: the pack's excess over near-lossless is **14.3 % smaller** than OG's
1.00 b/w row, at **1.1788 b/w** — 18 % more bits. At 1.18 b/w their three published points
(1.00→1.78, 0.80→2.12, 0.55→2.86) interpolate to **1.35–1.58**, so 1.5254 sits inside the band near
its top: **at equal rate the screen does not separate the two artifacts.** This is the screen, not
the confirm list, and OG's row is the full test set against five windows.

## Against Q2

`1.462553` is **2.50 % under** the 1.5 official bar. The 17-window v0 estimate was `1.417631`
`[1.355945, 1.482123]`; this screen is inside that interval. Against bartowski's Q4 the same
numerator is `1.462553 / 0.96826 = 1.5105` if the measured bar factor carries — **0.70 % over** the
bar. The PPL half is therefore still bar-dependent, and the v1 bartowski arm has not run.

The 0-shot half is unchanged: **0.7534×** against 0.90, six tasks × 100 items, v0. The pack fails Q2
on that half whatever the PPL arm says.
