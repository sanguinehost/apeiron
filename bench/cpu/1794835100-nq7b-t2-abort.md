# 7B T=2 stopped at 12 of 196 sections: cost and host instability, not a located regression

Criterion: **Q1** (a full 7B pack at 1.10 b/w) and **Q2** (its PPL against Q4_K_M). Grade:
`measured` for the stop facts, the per-linear walls, the hashes and the spend. No pack and no PPL
exist, so neither criterion is closed. Nothing here is a quality claim about T=2.

## Result

No 7B pack, no 7B PPL and no Q2 ratio exist. The fit was stopped after 12 saved sections (25
linears produced) because the four-hour spend cap at $0.222/h cannot cover the measured 5.2–6.3 h
fit, one host rebooted twice, and the cheaper fallback hosts refused SSH. This receipt first
recorded a different cause — "the fitter is invalid" — and that cause is **withdrawn**: the 0.6B
refits it cited used the example's **column-RMS** fallback, while the reference pipeline and this
7B run use the **activation Gram diagonal** (`--diag-dir diag-c6x29`). That comparison is
confounded (`bench/cpu/1794834689-nq06-l0-1174.md`, erratum), so no tree regression is established
and the 12 saved sections are on the reference path. What the stop does establish is that the
column-RMS driver path may not be scored without `--diag-dir`.

## The stop

No `nq_fit` process was left running. Instance `60924353` had already been destroyed after its
second reboot; instance `60928180` (the fallback i9) never accepted SSH, so no fit was launched on
it. Both are gone. `vastai show instances` after the destroy lists only `60928665`, another
session's instance, not this task's.

The PPL arms for both skeletons were not run. No 7B quality number exists.

## What this does not cover

- No 7B pack, no 7B PPL, no Q2 ratio, and no Q1 rate closure. There is no 7B quality number.
- The preconditioner confound is not resolved: the 0.6B column-RMS arm is pathological, the
  activation-diagonal arm has not been re-run, and the 12 saved 7B sections are unscored. No `src/`
  change was made.
- The per-linear walls are from a 7.68-CPU i7 slice and a 9900X host that rebooted twice; they are
  not a 7B rate claim and not evidence about a stable host.
- The uniform 1.10M ppm plan and the 3.0 b/w layer-0 plan were not executed. The layer-0 plan
  remains blocked by the dense sign-factor cap (1.19 b/w at 3584×18944, 2.0 at square 3584×3584);
  `nq_fit` writes no residual.
- The Q6_K head comparison, 0-shot, 200k context, decode rate and Vulkan were not touched.
- The T=2 arm was stopped for cost and host instability, not because T=2 was judged
  underconverged. A converged or underconverged verdict for T=2 needs a completed full pack first;
  T=2 remains an upper bound on the converged ratio by construction.
