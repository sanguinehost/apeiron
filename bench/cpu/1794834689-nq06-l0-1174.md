# The 0.6B layer-0 1.174 b/w point: the current fitter gives 185,327 PPL, 10,228× Q4

> **Erratum (17 Nov, before the sun): the tree-regression reading is withdrawn.** Both refits in
> this receipt used `examples/nq_fit`'s fallback **column-RMS** preconditioner (`col_rms`), because
> no `--diag-dir` was supplied. The 0.977441 control pack comes from the **reference pipeline**,
> whose `i_norm` is the **activation Gram diagonal** `g{l}.{tag}.{gram}.diag.i64`
> (`tests/learn_nanoquant.rs::nq_fit`; `bench/notes/nq-rate-1174.md` §7: "the probe's
> preconditioner is the tensor's column RMS, not the activation Gram diagonal the reference
> pipeline uses"). The 21,449× gap at the same rate therefore compares two different
> preconditioners; it does not establish a `fro2` regression, and the `relF` bands are weight-space
> numbers from the column-RMS arm only. What stands: the **column-RMS driver path** produces
> unusable packs at both rates, so no fit without `--diag-dir` may be scored; the 1.174 point is
> **unmeasured**; D6 is **untested, not refuted**. The next measurement is a 0.977441 fit on the
> activation diagonal that reproduces ≈57.045 before any 1.174 point is quoted. The rows below are
> correct as measurements of the column-RMS arm.

Criterion: **Q2** (wikitext-2 perplexity against the same checkpoint's Q4_K_M). Grade: `measured`
for every PPL, fit wall and rate below; `model` for the ratios and any inference about a rate the
fits do not reach. Nothing here is `proved`. The deliverable — a usable layer-0 rate/quality point
at 1.174 b/w — is **not reached**: the fit at 1.174M ppm lands at 1.153288 b/w and scores
**185,326.710597** against Q4's 18.118871, while the 0.977441 b/w control on the same arm and box
scores **57.114113**. The 1.174 fit is 3,245× *worse* than the lower-rate control, so this is a
fit-path failure (the column-RMS arm), not a rate result, and it does not close D6.

## The control, and what it is

The 0.977441 control pack is no longer on disk as a file. It is reproduced here from the committed
`nq06-v3.nqp` (196 sections, 53,815,472 B): the seven layer-0 sections extracted byte for byte with
`extract_l0.py` (`APNQPK01` v3 → v2 subset) give 1,921,984 B, 1,921,728 factor bytes, ranks
q/o 640, k/v 480, gate/up/down 736, and print **1.921728e6 bytes / 15,728,640 weights = 0.977441
b/w** — the control log's own numbers. Scored on this box: **57.114113**, against the laptop's
57.045080 (+0.12%). The difference is a reader build (the working-tree `src/engine/kern/nq.rs` was
rewritten at 21:53 on 16 Nov; the laptop measurement predates it), not the stream: Q4 is identical
on both hosts.

The control's ranks are the pre-`-16` `rank_for_bits` output. The current function at the same
0.977441M ppm gives gate/up/down rank **704**, not 736, so the control sections were fitted by an
earlier tree — the November 12 v3 campaign — and not by the current `examples/nq_fit`.

## Two controls on the failure

- **Reader.** The laptop's `target/release/examples/ppl` build of 16 Nov 19:50 (before the 21:53
  reader rewrite) scores the 1.153288 pack at **185,326.710597**, mean nll 12.129876, 29.6 tok/s,
  69.0 s — the box's current build's number to the last digit. The served reader is not the cause.
- **Skeleton.** On the F16 skeleton the pack is 147,340.550714 against F16's 16.609504
  (8,870.9×); the Q4 skeleton is not the cause either.

The fits' Frobenius error is `relF` 0.48–0.62 at both rates, so the failure is not a visible
energy deficit: the factors are close in total energy and pathological in the directions the
network reads. The `admm_loss` (0.22–0.35) is also in the same band at both rates.

## D6

Plainly: **D6 is not supported by this arm, and it is not refuted either.** The 1.174 arm is
3,245× *further* from Q4 than the 0.978 control, not closer, so raising layer 0's rate did not cut
the loss in the artifact that exists. That artifact is not a rate result: the control was fitted by
the reference pipeline on the activation Gram diagonal, while both refits here used the example's
column-RMS fallback, and the same-rate refit is 21,449× worse. The preconditioner, not the rate,
owns the gap (see the erratum at the top). The 1.174 rate remains unmeasured; the next measurement
is a 0.977441 fit on the activation diagonal that reproduces 57.045 before any 1.174 point is
quoted, then the same fit at 1.174.

## What this does not cover

- The 7B model. This is the 0.6B layer-0 proxy; no 7B pack or PPL exists here.
- The full model. A partial pack of seven layer-0 linears on a Q4 skeleton; layers 1–27 keep their
  Q4_K_M weights, so this is a layer control, not a model result.
- The head. Both arms serve Q4_K_M's tied `Q6_K` token embedding, so this arm has **no** `Q8_0`
  head confound; the full v3 pack's 74.297 and 2.52× rows used the `Q8_0` head skeleton and are not
  head-matched to this table.
- 2,044 tokens at ctx 512 (and 511 in the short row). No 8,192-token row, no ctx ≥ 2,048, no 0-shot
  task suite.
- The 1.30 b/w arm. It was not run: a higher-rate fit from a fitter that fails at 1.174 has no
  interpretation, and the step-6 condition ("if the 1.174 PPL is still high") is true only because
  the fit is broken.
- The `nq_fit` doc-comment rank set (`q:800,k:608,v:608,o:800,gate:864,up:864,down:864`,
  1.174251 b/w). It shares gate/up/down rank 864 with the 1.153288 pack above, so the failure is
  not confined to the +32 ranks on q/k/v/o; fitting that set at T=400 was not a separate arm.
- The exact reader at full length. The `--kernel exact` arm was stopped at 15 min (≈2 tok/s); the
  old-binary A/B already rules out the q8 reader.
- Why the current fitter is pathological. The `relF` and `admm_loss` bands are stated, not
  explained; no factor, Gram or activation inspection was run.
- rustc/LLVM code generation, the kernel ABI, Vulkan/SPIR-V, the GPU and the `unsafe` container.
