# `Q6_K` folds into the shared loop

Criterion: **T2** (prefill route), one projection path. Grade: `measured` for the paired wall,
`tested` for equality. Host: ThinkPad L13 Yoga, i5-10210U (4C/8T), 6.6 GiB, `performance`, no
AVX-VNNI, measured 27.97 GB/s. This receipt advances T2's prefill routing; it does not close T2.

## Change

`matmat_q8act` took `chart::gemm_q6_k` before the shared loop. The pre-dispatch is deleted;
`Q6_K` now enters `fast_family` and takes `pooled`'s packed eight-column lane like `Q4_K`.

- `gemm_q6_k`, `Q6KRows`, `Q6_K_FAST_TOP`, `ROWS_PER_TASK`, `NARROW_ROWS_PER_TASK`,
  `NARROW_ROWS`, `task_rows`, `ColWrites` and `empty_columns` are removed from `chart::kcols`.
  Their last caller was `gemm_q6_k`; the pool machinery they used stays in `chart::pool`.
- `Q6KRow`, `q6_k_row_lanes_avx2` and `pack_cols(qs, blocks · 8, false)` stay. The lane reads the
  same packed panel as `Q4_K` and keeps its exact `i128` fold as the past-`limit` fallback.
- The shared admission is wider on the bound and capped on columns: `k ≤ 4096`, the wide
  `fast_bound_ok` branch, `n_in % 256`, and the same `pack_cols` power-of-two rule. `gemm_q6_k`
  had no explicit column bound but used the narrow bound and its own task split. `k = 1`, calls
  above `k = 4096`, and calls the lane declines take the per-column contract.

## Equality

- Paired test, before timing each `k`: `pooled(Q6_K)` equals `gemm_q6_k` word for word at
  `n_out = n_in = 3584`, `k ∈ {32, 128, 512}`. It passed in all three processes below; the test
  was deleted with `gemm_q6_k`.
- `pooled_q6_k_matches_per_column`: 3×512 and 2×1024 payloads, `k ∈ {2, 8, 33, 64}`; `pooled`,
  `matmat_q8act` and every column of `matvec_q8act` agree. `tested`, exit 0.
- `q6_k_matmat_equals_the_per_column_contract` (retargeted from `gemm_q6_k`): `n_out = 7`,
  `n_in ∈ {256, 1024}`, `k ∈ {1, 3, 8, 9, 33}`, narrow and wide-bound columns, non-finite scale,
  past-bound column, ragged 992-wide input. `tested`, exit 0. It also asserts the wide columns
  take the `i128` fold and the narrow ones stay in `i64` lanes.
- `q6_k_matmat` in `bench_call_arms` now times the surviving `matmat_q8act` route instead of the
  deleted entry.

The first run is not admissible as the paired result: its `k = 128` pooled arm has a 43.085 ms mean
against a 15.981 ms best, so one of the three passes was contended, and the guard log recorded two
queued heavy jobs at start. The decision uses reruns A and B, both taken with the guard reporting no
heavy process. Every per-`k` ratio there is inside `[0.85, 1.15]`; the range is 0.89–1.11.

The mean is a worse estimator here than the paired best: rerun A's pooled means are
3.748 / 30.033 / 99.351 ms against gemm's 3.622 / 14.476 / 94.534 ms, and rerun B's are
3.633 / 12.532 / 100.212 ms against 3.727 / 43.956 / 102.231 ms. The cold pass lands on either arm
and moves the mean by more than the route difference. The same estimator on the same binary moved
`k = 128` from 1.02 to 0.89 between the two idle runs; that 0.13 is the observed spread, and the
carrier difference is inside it.

## What remains

- The `Q6_K` packed lane has no Aeneas-verified safe twin. `Q6KRow::fill` and
  `q6_k_row_lanes_avx2` restate arithmetic that `q6_k_row_kcols_avx2` / `q6_k_row_fast` also carry;
  the equality to `matvec_q8act` is `tested`, not `proved`. It is `tested` debt under `AGENTS.md`
  `## Unsafe`, the same status as the `Q4_K` packed lane.
- `k = 1` and `k > MAX_POOLED_K = 4096` take the per-column contract. The production prefill block
  is 256; no receipt measures a Q6_K call above 4096 columns.
- The paired test is deleted with `gemm_q6_k`, so no Q6_K production-shape rate test survives.
  Rerunning `pooled_q6_k_matches_per_column` is the equality gate; the logs are
  `/tmp/opencode/q6k-paired.log`, `/tmp/opencode/q6k-paired-rerun.log` and
  `/tmp/opencode/q6k-paired-rerun2.log`.
- The wall is a synthetic 3584² payload on a shared laptop. No end-to-end 7B prefill or turn ran
  with this carrier.
- The maintainer extraction/Lean gate (`proof/regen.sh --check`, `lake build`) was not run. No
  `chart::kcols` declaration appears in the committed extraction, so this change adds no extracted
  body and no Lean claim.
- The pooled `Q8_0` AVX-VNNI entry is still a sibling in front of the shared loop.
- Not covered: rustc/LLVM code generation, the kernel ABI, AVX-VNNI (absent on this host), the GPU.
