# Apeiron Word Refinement Proofs

Aeneas extraction and refinement proofs for the Apeiron `Cell<i64>` kernels. Two Lake packages,
so the machine-word extraction and the corpus link can be built and trusted separately:

| Package | Directory | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `apeironWordProof` | `proof/` | Extract the kernels and define their `ZMod (2^64)` denotation |
| `apeironWordBridge` | `proof/corpus/` | Compile the canonical corpus and prove the refinement link against `AnyRing` |

Neither is imported by the `apeiron` crate.

This file is the local proof authority for the extraction and bridge under `proof/`. The programme
thesis, ownership, outcomes, primitive correspondence, migration and execution order are in the
[programme direction](../docs/direction.md); that document is the programme authority. The physical
canonical corpus remains at `../sanguine-apeiron/proof/Apeiron/` with unchanged pins. The table
claim's scope and trust boundary are in the [local boundary](../docs/kernel-boundary.md).

## Purpose

Apeiron is the O(1)-primitive basis at fixed N with bounded callbacks and nonconstant key length,
evolved from hashtrinity; `fold/hashtrinity` is the naive predecessor. The basis is
`R[x]/(x^2 - sigma)`, `Cell<R>` with `Sigma` as a value in every executing signature, `Coord`
addressing correct per face, and the executing growable `Table`: a flat certified-coordinate
resident area plus grouped spill regions, keyed by `(Sigma, K)` with `Stored`/`Regen` carriers. The
historical fixed-shard `Table<K, V, H, N>` (one home shard, `ShardFull` refusal, `N = 0` refusal)
and its bounded-admission proofs are revision-bound historical evidence, not proofs of the executing
layout. Every executing face refines the canonical object; anything not rooted in it is not
accepted. The canonical object is
`Apeiron.Table.{HMap,get,insertWith,build}` and
`Apeiron.Trichotomy.AnyRing.{RingCell,ringMul,ringNormForm}` with the extracted `Cell<i64>` word
bodies (`word_mul_denote`, `word_norm_denote`).

The O(1) qualifier is the parameter boundary. Fixed N, bounded callbacks and nonconstant key length
bound the table; reading a whole source remains at least linear in its bytes at fixed N, so
constant-time table operations at fixed N do not imply constant-time whole-source checking or
constant-size proof terms.

### The checked-claim kernel

`accept_derived_impulse` selects its branch with an explicit `if` on the derived `Cell` equality.
Charon therefore translates the equality and the branch rather than the external
`core.bool.Bool.then` combinator, and `regen.sh`'s audit stays clean: the generated file contains no
`axiom`. The generated equality `cell.Cell.Insts.CoreCmpPartialEqCell.eq` compares the two word
slots in turn, so the acceptance obligation is decided by source-word equality; the corpus theorems
below prove the exact acceptance/rejection behaviour without reading `ZMod (2^64)` equality back as
word equality.

### Point density and flux refinement

`word_point_density` and `word_point_flux` are the extracted `i64` specialisations of the real
`src/carrier.rs` generic kernels, which build `(amplitude, 0)`, multiply by the direction and take
the norm form (`point_density`), then multiply by the forward phase difference
(`point_flux`). `WordRefinement.lean` proves:

- `word_norm_of_mul_denote`: the shared step, composing the extracted `word_mul` and `word_norm`
  refinements to give the canonical `ringNormForm` of the canonical `ringMul` product;
- `word_point_density_denote`: extracted `word_point_density` = `Apeiron.Physics.pointDensity` at
  the symbolic dial `denoteSigma s`;
- `word_point_flux_denote`: extracted `word_point_flux` = `Apeiron.Physics.pointFlux` at
  `denoteSigma s`, composing the density refinement with the wrapping `sub`/`mul` denotations;
- `word_point_density_eq`: transfer of the canonical `pointDensity_eq` factorisation into the
  amplitude's and direction's norm forms;
- `word_point_density_ringOne` / `word_point_density_ringOne_independent` and
  `word_point_flux_ringOne` / `word_point_flux_ringOne_independent`: the unit-direction
  specialisations, where the dial drops out, matching the canonical `pointDensity_ringOne` /
  `pointFlux_ringOne` and their independence theorems;
- `word_point_density_total`: the density kernel is total.

All are total `ok` equalities for every full-width input and every dial value, with no `sorry`.
The denotation theorems compose `word_mul_denote` and `word_norm_denote`; they do not restate the
polynomial.

### Scalar carrier step and exact-half flux

`word_conservative_density_step` and `word_diffusive_flux_half` are the extracted `i64`
specialisations of the real `src/carrier.rs` kernels. Both are **partial**: an intermediate that
does not fit `i64`, or an odd face difference, is refused as `ok none`. `WordRefinement.lean`
proves, over `ZMod (2 ^ 64)`:

- `word_conservative_density_step_denote`: a successful extracted step denotes
  `Apeiron.Physics.scalarCarrierStep` at the denotation of its four `i64` arguments;
- `word_conservative_density_step_eq_some_iff`: the step succeeds exactly when all three generated
  checked intermediates (`outgoing - incoming`, `density - net`, `after + source`) are `some` — the
  exact generated no-overflow precondition;
- `word_conservative_density_step_success`: under those three preconditions the step succeeds and
  denotes the canonical `scalarCarrierStep`;
- `word_diffusive_flux_half_denote`: a successful extracted half-flux satisfies the canonical
  `Apeiron.Physics.exactHalfFlux` witness `2 * f = left - right`;
- `word_diffusive_flux_half_success`: under representability (`right - left ∈ [i64::MIN, i64::MAX]`)
  and evenness of the face difference, the kernel succeeds and the result is that witness;
- `word_diffusive_flux_half_odd_refusal`, `word_diffusive_flux_half_unrepresentable_refusal` and
  `word_conservative_density_step_overflow_refusal`: concrete inputs on which the kernels return
  `ok none`, so partiality is real and no theorem claims totality.

The denotation proofs read the generated `I64.checked_sub`/`checked_add`/`checked_rem`/`checked_div`
specs and carry each result through `denote`; they do not restate the arithmetic. The `?` operator
is not used in these two kernels because it desugars through `Try::branch`, which Aeneas
has no model for; the source uses explicit `match` with early `return None` instead. No claim is
made that every integer state is iterable or that the updated value remains a norm-form square.

### The three-cell diffusive line

`word_diffusive_line3_step` is the extracted `i64` specialisation of the real
`src/carrier.rs::diffusive_line3_step`: a closed three-cell line with `nu = 1/2`. The two interior
faces are read once each from `word_diffusive_flux_half`, the two endpoint faces are fixed at zero,
and the three cells are updated by `word_conservative_density_step`. Every local is computed before
the result is constructed, so a late refusal returns `ok none` with no partially updated line.
`carrier::DensityLine3` is a plain `Copy`/`Eq` triple; the extracted `carrier.DensityLine3` is the
generated struct. `WordRefinement.lean` proves:

- `word_diffusive_flux_half_cases` / `word_conservative_density_step_cases`: each partial kernel
  either succeeds with a word or returns `ok none`, the case split the composition needs;
- `word_diffusive_line3_step_eq_some_iff`: the step returns `some next` exactly when both faces
  exist, all three checked cell updates succeed, and `next` is their componentwise result;
- `word_diffusive_line3_step_denote_of` / `word_diffusive_line3_step_denote`: on success the two
  interior faces are `Apeiron.Physics.exactHalfFlux` witnesses and each updated component is the
  canonical `Apeiron.Physics.scalarCarrierStep` at the shared face and closed end;
- `word_diffusive_line3_step_total_mass`: the denoted total changes only by the denoted sources,
  proved by instantiating the existing `word_conservative_total_mass`, not a second telescoping;
- `word_diffusive_line3_step_total_mass_zero_source`: the zero-source conservation corollary;
- `word_diffusive_line3_step_zero_success`: the all-zero old/source line returns the all-zero line as
  `some`, so the success branch is inhabited and the partial kernel is not vacuously total;
- `word_diffusive_line3_step_odd_refusal`: the odd interior difference `1 - 0` refuses the whole
  line, so the step is not total;
- `word_diffusive_line3_step_unrepresentable_refusal`: with all three cells at `i64::MAX`, the
  interior differences are even (faces `0`), the left cell succeeds, and the centre cell's
  `after_net + source` (`i64::MAX + 1`) is unrepresentable, so the whole line returns `ok none` — a
  line-level unrepresentable refusal distinct from the odd-face one.

The composition is a `ZMod (2 ^ 64)` statement over the three-cell line, not a physical mass law;
it inherits the partiality and the no-partial-result shape of the two scalar kernels.

The corpus now carries the whole-line ring statement this refines. `d9aefc93` adds
`Apeiron.Physics.diffusiveUpdate_half_three_anyRing`: at the explicit `DoublesTo ν 1` witness and a
closed right ghost face, the two shared faces are `exactHalfFlux` witnesses, each cell averages with
its neighbor and the three-cell total is conserved — the ring analogue of the real
`CarrierDissipation.diffusiveUpdate_half_three`. At `ZMod (2 ^ 64)` there is no `ν` with
`2 * ν = 1`: reducing `2 * ν = 1` modulo `2` gives `0 = 1`. So `DoublesTo ν 1` is unsatisfiable and
the ring theorem is vacuous at the bridge's ring. The extracted-word refinement above therefore
carries the face value
itself rather than a scalar half: the two interior faces are `exactHalfFlux` witnesses
(`2 * f = left - right`) and each component is `scalarCarrierStep`. That is the non-vacuous
extracted counterpart, not a restatement of the corpus theorem.

No energy claim crosses into the word ring. The corpus proves the exact real identity
`dirichletEnergy_halfStep_three` (`E' = (1/4) * E` at `ν = 1/2` with the right ghost face closed)
and its strict form `dirichletEnergy_halfStep_three_lt` for positive energy; the factor `1/4` needs
the order of `ℝ`, so `diffusiveUpdate_half_three_anyRing` and this bridge make no such claim.

### Exchange refinement

`Apeiron.Physics.CarrierBodyWorkAnyRing` (corpus) carries the equal-mass exchange to an arbitrary
commutative ring, so `Z/2^64` is an instance rather than an analogy. It defines `ringExchangeImpulse`,
`ringCarrierMomentumAfter`, `ringBodyMomentumAfter` and `ringNormFormLedger` on
`Apeiron.Trichotomy.AnyRing.RingCell R`, and proves:

- `ringCarrierMomentumAfter_eq` / `ringBodyMomentumAfter_eq`: the exchange swaps the two momenta;
- `ringExchangeImpulse_antisymm`: the impulse is equal and opposite;
- `ringTotalMomentum_conserved`: the total basis momentum is unchanged;
- `ringNormFormLedger_conserved`: the `ringNormForm` ledger is unchanged at every dial value.

This is the norm-form ledger, not kinetic energy; `ringNormForm` is indefinite in a general ring and
no nonnegativity is claimed. The real-valued `Apeiron.Physics.CarrierBodyWork` is **not** transferred
to wrapping words by name; the ring-generic file is the specification the wrapping words refine.

`WordRefinement.lean` links the extracted `i64` kernels to it:

- `word_exchange_impulse_denote`: extracted `word_exchange_impulse` = `ringExchangeImpulse`;
- `word_elastic_exchange_denote`: extracted `word_elastic_exchange` = the canonical impulse,
  carrier and body fields;
- `word_elastic_exchange_carrier` / `word_elastic_exchange_body`: transfer of the swap;
- `word_elastic_exchange_totalMomentum`: transfer of total-momentum conservation;
- `word_elastic_exchange_normFormLedger`: transfer of ledger conservation at every symbolic
  `denoteSigma s`;
- `word_exchange_impulse_antisymm`: transfer of impulse antisymmetry;
- `word_accept_derived_impulse_spec`: the checked-claim kernel returns `some` exactly when the
  claim equals the derived word impulse (a single iff);
- `word_accept_derived_impulse_rejects` / `word_accept_derived_impulse_rejects_of_ne`: any claim
  unequal to the derived word impulse is rejected with `ok none`;
- `word_accept_derived_impulse_accepts` / `word_accept_derived_impulse_accepts_derived`: the exact
  derived word impulse is accepted and returns the elastic exchange;
- `word_accept_derived_impulse_denote` / `word_accept_derived_impulse_denote_canonical`: the
  accepted exchange denotes exactly what `word_elastic_exchange` denotes, and through the existing
  `word_elastic_exchange_denote` the canonical ring exchange impulse and momentum updates.

All are total `ok` equalities for every full-width input and every dial value, with no `sorry`.
The acceptance proofs select the branch by source-word equality of the two `I64` slots
(`word_cell_eq_eq`) and carry the result over with `word_elastic_exchange_denote`; they never infer
word equality from `ZMod (2^64)` equality.

`FunsExternal`/`TypesExternal` model file is the intended Aeneas hook (`-split-files` emits those
templates; filling a hole replaces its `axiom`), or a Std extension; appropriate for effectful
operations over the existing representation. **Removing the root is not a fourth option:** it does
not discharge the obligation, it deletes the extracted artifact and the refinement theorem. A
removed root is recorded as an **explicit transfer** to a named, testable premise, never presented as
a resolution.

**Cost determination for the three responses, and the decision taken.** (a) capacity-carrying `Vec`
model — runtime cost **zero by construction** (the executing `src/` is untouched; only the Lean model
changes) and **landed**: the model patch plus `strip-modeled-axioms.py` give 0 axioms over all 43
roots and `regen.sh --check` green, so the write/growth path stays in the cone with a refinement
theorem; the capacity-carrying patch surface is counted (one `Aeneas/Std/Vec.lean`), and the
unavoidable cost is that a model patch invalidates the backend build that `proof/lakefile.lean`
`require`s, paid once per model revision. Feasibility was established by the spike receipt
(`docs/evidence/vec-capacity-model-spike.md`, `docs/evidence/vec-capacity-model.patch`), not assumed. Its residual
trust boundary: the model carries capacity and the reservation outcome, so "the real allocator
conforms" is one named premise (`ResourceAdapterSimulates`), while `size_of` stays a layout
assumption. (b) parameterize/hoist allocation — runtime uncertain, the nearest experiment
(`one-allocation`, `f78ce71`) measured **not a net win** (58 win / 13 tie / 37 loss against the
baseline's 58 / 18 / 30). (c) bounded-fiber, no allocation — provable by construction at the
recorded `256·N` ceiling and reduced admission, i.e. the growable design was selected *for*
performance, so this is a design reversal.

Historical precedent that bounded the expectation: the fixed-shard `SafeTable<K,V,N>` was extracted

#### Rebuilding the patched translator

The `aeneas` binary is OCaml and is not in the archive, which ships only the release build. The
working translator is the pinned sources plus `proof/patches/`:

```sh

proof/install-patches.sh --aeneas-src "$PWD/aeneas"

# 4. an AENEAS_HOME carrying that binary beside charon, charon-driver, rust-toolchain and the
#    archive's backends/lean (the unpatched one; step 2 of the route below patches the model copy)
```

Step 3 yields `84f9c1d2…` **only** at the source path `/home/socol/oc-tmp/aeneas-build/aeneas`. OCaml

### Patched tool sources

`proof/patches/` holds **three** patches, not two: two are source changes to the tool build, and the
third is a Lean-library change to the backend that the patched binaries never compile in.

| patch | base | applied by | rebuilds binaries |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `patches/charon-repeat-optional-trait.patch` | Charon `b104e24fea7d721b71e6c39fd70f26ff20bc0980`, tree `784a177101bbfd956047ac2ee1b7335d4c057b7b` | `install-patches.sh` | yes |
| `patches/aeneas-array-repeat.patch` | Aeneas `505b6ca35217e7be5c96c3e2f8045edfbdf47291`, tree `af23d5d9d866050c2158700cedab07501443bcdd` | `install-patches.sh` | yes |
| `patches/aeneas-lean-model.patch` | installed `backends/lean` from the pinned archive above (no git base) | `model-patches.sh` | no |

`patches/PINS` records, for each of the two upstream bases, the commit, the git tree and the sha256
of every base file the patch modifies — 9 base files, 6 under Charon and 3 under Aeneas — plus the
sha256 of each patch file and the expected `aeneas` digest. The two tool-build patch files are the
exact `git diff` of the source working trees the patched binaries were built from; patch sha256
`c6099150efe8416a0495205a6bdf23925b9c79f2ecd6ca7a25335d0ae89e21c9` (Charon) and
`35bc103b490d7f38609c70e202eed365ab4a67a31634c14aa06186032ea1fcb1` (Aeneas). The Aeneas patch
carries three components, all in `Interp.ml` and `InterpStatements.ml`:

| component | file | effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `to_extract` fold | `Interp.ml`, +12 | `core::array::repeat` is translated whether or not Charon groups it |
| `fun_info` registration | `Interp.ml`, +33 | registers that function's effects, **guarded on absence**, so a crate that does group it is not registered twice |
| native `Repeat` lowering | `InterpStatements.ml`, +68 | a `const` element operand with no `Copy` clause lowers to `Array.repeat` |

The middle component is the one earlier removed here on the stated ground that "the function is already
in the crate's declaration groups, so that pass registered the same `Fun` id twice". That ground is
false for this crate: with the component absent the translator dies at
`symbolic/SymbolicToPureTypes.ml:724`, which is the failure the guard cannot cause and the
unconditional registration would also have caused. The guard is `when not (FunOrMethodId.Map.mem
(FunOrMethodId.Fun id) acc.infos)`, so the duplicate-registration assert is unreachable. The three
components are adjacent insertions in one `let` chain, so `git apply` carries them as one hunk per
file; the split is here, not in the diff.

The model patch is not listed in `PINS` and `install-patches.sh` does not apply it: its base is the
unpacked archive, not a git checkout, so the archive digest above is what pins it. It touches 8 files
under `Aeneas/Std` (`Vec.lean`, `String.lean`, `Core/{Cmp,CoreOption,CoreResult}.lean`,
`Scalar/{Casts,Ops}.lean`, `Scalar/WrappingOps/Shl.lean`); sha256
`9ad11fb71e05aae223719c142d7e70f4a8aa5c8afa4d91ccfd8554c8e2f854cf`.

`Scalar/Casts.lean` also carries the two names the pinned release declares nowhere and the extraction
reaches. `F32` is what the pinned emitter prints for Rust `f32` (`float_name` in
`src/extract/ExtractBase.ml`), declared there as the opaque value `core.f32.F32` with the bare
`abbrev F32`; the model gives it no operation, so `core::f32::f32::is_finite`,
`mixer.f32_carrier.q32_to_f32`, `attn.alloc_f32_storage` and `attn.KvState.attn_head_f32` stay
`axiom`s of the extracted fragment and are admitted by
[`boundary-axioms.txt`](boundary-axioms.txt). Nothing numeric is read off that type: a claim needing
the meaning of a `f32` is proved in the corpus against that meaning.
`I128.Insts.CoreConvertFromU64.from` is the `u64 as i128` cast the attention fragment reaches, a
zero-extending widening that cannot lose a value. Both are in this one patch because
`model-patches.sh` rebuilds `target/aeneas-model/lean` from it; an addition left only in that
untracked directory is lost on the next run.

`install-patches.sh` locates a source checkout, verifies its `HEAD` commit, git tree, the sha256 of
each patch file and the base file digests against `PINS`, then checks or applies both patches. The
Charon source defaults to `$AENEAS_SRC/charon`. Aeneas source resolution is `--aeneas-src`, then
`APEIRON_AENEAS_SRC`, then `AENEAS_SRC`, then `AENEAS_HOME` and `target/aeneas` if those are git
checkouts. `target/aeneas/` is the installed binary tree, not a source checkout, so a source argument
is required unless the sources are checked out elsewhere.

```sh
proof/install-patches.sh --check --aeneas-src /path/to/aeneas-src
proof/install-patches.sh --aeneas-src /path/to/aeneas-src
```

`--check` archives the pinned `HEAD` tree with `git archive` into a temporary directory and runs
`git apply --check` there, so it neither reads nor writes the source working tree and is independent
of local edits. It exits nonzero on a base commit, tree, patch-file or file-digest mismatch, and on a
hunk that does not apply. Install mode applies the patches to the checkout and skips a patch already
applied; the two modes were exercised against a fresh checkout of both pinned revisions, and the
applied working-tree diff is byte-identical to the patch files. Install mode's idempotence test is
per patch *file*, so a checkout with one file of a two-file patch reverted is refused rather than
repaired.

### Route from a clean checkout to a built `proof/`

`proof/lakefile.lean:19` is `require aeneas from "../target/aeneas-model/lean"`, and the committed
`proof/lake-manifest.json:9` records `"dir": "../target/aeneas-model/lean"` for that package. `/target`
is gitignored, so a clean checkout has neither `target/aeneas/` nor `target/aeneas-model/`, and the
order is fixed:

| step | command | what performs it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. install the pinned tools | unpack the archive above into `target/aeneas/`, then **replace `target/aeneas/aeneas` with the patched build** by the route in "Rebuilding the patched translator", or point `AENEAS_HOME` at an installation carrying it | manual unpack plus `install-patches.sh` and `dune build`; the archive's own `aeneas` cannot read the patched Charon's LLBC. `regen.sh:18` defaults `AENEAS_HOME` to `target/aeneas` and, when it is set elsewhere, `regen.sh:19-24` symlinks `target/aeneas` to it |
| 2. materialise the patched Lean model | `proof/model-patches.sh` | copies `${AENEAS_HOME:-$REPO_DIR/target/aeneas}/backends/lean` to `target/aeneas-model/lean` and applies `patches/aeneas-lean-model.patch` with `patch -p1 --forward` |
| 3. extract, audit and build `proof/` | `proof/regen.sh`, or `proof/regen.sh --check` | extraction reads `LEAN_BACKEND="$AENEAS_HOME/backends/lean"` (`regen.sh:29`), the unpatched installed backend; its `lake build` (`regen.sh:270`) resolves the `aeneas` path dependency from step 2 |
| 4. build the bridge | `cd proof/corpus && lake build` | `proof/corpus/lakefile.lean` |

Step 2 precedes step 3 because `lake` resolves `../target/aeneas-model/lean` before any module is
built, and `regen.sh` does not create it. `model-patches.sh` parses no arguments: it has no `--help`
and no `--check`, and it always removes and rebuilds the destination. It symlinks
`proof/.lake/packages` to `target/aeneas-model/lean/.lake/packages`; `ln -sfn` succeeds on an absent
target, so the symlink is created before step 3 populates that directory.

The model exists because the pinned release does not model the externals the growable table reaches
and carries no `Vec` capacity: `proof/lakefile.lean:14-18` lists them (`Vec` capacity,
`try_reserve`/`try_reserve_exact`/`truncate`/`clear`, `Ord::max` taking its `Ord` instance, and
`Option::{as_ref,Try::branch,Try::from_residual}` / `Result::is_err`). Extraction runs against the
unpatched backend and the model supplies the missing Lean definitions, so step 2 changes the built
package, not the generated file. `proof/strip-modeled-axioms.py`, run by `regen.sh` after generation,
removes the axiom stubs the model now supplies.

Run Lean from the package directory, as `cd proof/corpus && lake build`; never `lake -d` from a
directory without that package's `lean-toolchain` (`AGENTS.md` §10).

## What this does not establish

- Not covered: callbacks outside `CallbackLawsG`, including implementations that allocate, panic or
  diverge. Generic refinement is proved only under those explicit laws and the stated admission
  predicates. Established for the concrete `I64`
  word instance: `PhysicalInv` constructor at every `N`, `insert_with` preservation at every `N`
  (exact `ShardFull` refusal at `N = 0`), exact `BuildAdmissibleN` build success/refusal with
  canonical ordered `ReadRep` for positive `N`, the physical `PhysicalInv` postcondition for
  successful positive-`N` build, and the exact zero-capacity build characterization. Also
  established: extracted
  table witnesses, scalar kernels, the two cell operations, the carrier point-density/flux readout,
  the scalar carrier step and exact-half flux, the closed three-cell diffusive line, the two
  arithmetic exchange kernels, the checked-claim acceptance kernel and the finite
  conservative-total composition.
- Totality of the scalar carrier step, the exact-half flux or the three-cell line. They refuse
  unrepresentable intermediates and odd face differences as `ok none`; the refinement theorems are
  conditional on the generated no-overflow preconditions and the representability/evenness
  hypotheses, and the refusal witnesses (`word_diffusive_line3_step_odd_refusal`,
  `word_diffusive_line3_step_unrepresentable_refusal`) show the partiality is real while
  `word_diffusive_line3_step_zero_success` shows the success branch is inhabited.
- That every integer state is iterable, or that an updated value remains a norm-form square.
- Any property of rustc/LLVM code generation, the generated machine code or a GPU backend.
- That the finite conservation theorem is a physical mass law: `word_conservative_total_mass` is
  the denoted `ZMod (2^64)` sum over the `n` cells, and it requires an actual `ok (some (result i))`
  for every one of the `n` calls, so it does not cover a run with a refused intermediate.
- That the bridge exercises every corpus module; the corpus pins `4.31.0` and builds as a whole,
  but the bridge imports the `AnyRing` closure and the scalar-step links, not the ordered `ℝ`
  physics.
- That the real-valued `Apeiron.Physics.CarrierBodyWork` theorem applies to wrapping words. The
  wrapping refinement is stated against the ring-generic `CarrierBodyWorkAnyRing`, which claims no
  nonnegativity and no kinetic-energy reading.
- That the real-valued nonnegativity or zero-iff reading of `Apeiron.Physics.Carrier` transfers to
  wrapping words. The point-density/flux refinement is stated against the ring-generic
  `Apeiron.Physics.CarrierAnyRing`, which carries no order and makes no such claim.
- Any energy statement over the word ring. The corpus's exact real quarter-energy identity
  `dirichletEnergy_halfStep_three` (`E' = (1/4) * E`) and its strict form
  `dirichletEnergy_halfStep_three_lt` are over `ℝ`; the ring analogue
  `diffusiveUpdate_half_three_anyRing` and this bridge claim no energy, nonnegativity or ordering.
