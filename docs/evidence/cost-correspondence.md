# Cost correspondence: the performance model and the quantisation model from one floor

Module: `proof/corpus/Perf/CostCorrespondence.lean` (`proved`, closed cone, audited in `proof/corpus/AxiomAudit.lean`).

The performance model predicts because it has a floor on the binding resource (`Perf.Roofline.timeLB`). The
quantisation model had none: it counted bits per weight. This module derives both floors from one lemma and states
the rows where the two models agree and where they differ.

## The correspondence

- **One floor.** `cover_bound`: a set covered by `|I|` cells of measure `≤ c` has measure `≤ |I|·c`. With the counting
  measure on bytes it is the roofline field bound (`bytes_floor` gives `⌈|B|/cap⌉ ≤ n`). With Haar measure on a
  `d`-dimensional space it is the covering converse (`covering_floor`: `r^d ≤ |I|·ε^d`).
- **Stiff dimension, not parameter count.** `stiff_floor`: if a 1-Lipschitz map sends the admissible weights onto
  a ball of a `k`-dimensional stiff space, any `ε`-codebook has `≥ (r/ε)^k` words. `k` is the stiff dimension; the
  ambient dimension does not enter.
- **Gauge and basis invariance.** A finite group `G` that preserves the function and the measure lowers the codebook by at
  most the factor `|G|` (`gauge_lift_bound`). Permutations of `n` channels cost `≤ b·n` bits when `n ≤ 2^b`
  (`perm_gauge_le`). A continuous exact gauge direction `x` (`xᵀFx = 0`, `F ⪰ 0`) leaves every quadratic error
  unchanged (`gauge_null`, via `Matrix.PosSemidef.dotProduct_mulVec_zero_iff`). Under any invertible reparametrisation
  `T`, `charpoly((TSTᵀ)((Tᵀ)⁻¹FT⁻¹)) = charpoly(SF)` (`spectrum_conj`): rotations, scale rebalance, and triangular
  feedback move the coder's gap to the floor, never the floor.
- **Composition and corrector.** Both models compose by a triangle inequality; the norm differs (ℓ∞ over fields, ℓ¹ over a
  serial chain, ℓ² over score samples), and so does the sign of the summands. Demands are non-negative counts and never
  cancel (`roofline_nocancel`); layer scores are signed and do (`kl_cancels`). Joint/sum below 1 is exactly a negative
  score correlation (`kl_pair_sub_iff`), and the optimal linear corrector `c = −⟪δ,p⟫/‖p‖²` leaves residual
  `‖δ‖² − ⟪δ,p⟫²/‖p‖² = −disc(δ,p)/‖p‖²` (`corrector_identity`, `corrector_residual`, `corrector_attained`,
  `corrector_zero_iff`).
- **Faces of the pipeline.** The two-score form `‖αv + βw‖²` has discriminant `≤ 0` (Cauchy–Schwarz,
  `score_form_disc_nonpos`): elliptic or parabolic, never hyperbolic. For any 2×2 operator step `M`, its trace-free part
  `N = M − (tr M / 2)·1` satisfies `N² = disc2 M · 1` (`disc2_sq`) and `a·1 + b·N` multiplies as the canonical
  `Apeiron.Trichotomy.mul (disc2 M)` on `(a, b)` (`face_mul_canonical`): Hadamard rotation lies on the elliptic face
  (`rotation_elliptic`, `disc2 = −sin²θ < 0`), unit-triangular LDL/GPTQ feedback lies on the parabolic face
  (`shear_parabolic`, `disc2 = 0`, `shear_nilpotent`), and column/activation rebalance `diag(s, s⁻¹)` lies on the
  hyperbolic face (`rebalance_hyperbolic`, `disc2 = ((s − s⁻¹)/2)² > 0`, `rebalance_preserves_product`).
- **Water level.** Both models spend a divisible resource against a level. Performance: work `x_i` on speed `s_i`
  finishing by `T` gives `Σx ≤ T·Σs` (`fluid_floor`), met with equality when every worker ends at `W/S`
  (`fluid_attained`). Quantisation: component `i` at rate `r_i` leaves `λ_i e^{−c r_i}`; an allocation whose active
  components sit at a level `θ` is optimal at its total rate (`waterfill_opt`, KKT through the tangent line of `exp`),
  the level allocation leaves `min(λ_i, θ)` per component (`waterfill_level`), and no allocation of that total rate
  does better (`waterfill_floor`). In the high-resolution regime (`θ ≤ λ_i`), `KL(R)/KL(R') = 4^{−(R−R')}`
  (`waterfill_highres`, `waterfill_exclusion`).
- **Metric.** With `G = U diag(g) Uᵀ`, `H = V diag(h) Vᵀ` and `D = U E Vᵀ`, the Kronecker form is diagonal:
  `tr(DᵀGDH) = Σ g_i h_j E_ij²` (`kron_diag`). Each rotated coefficient is one water-filling component of weight
  `g_i h_j`, over all tensors at once.

## What this says for the onebit target

- The rate floor is set by the stiff dimension of the function, not the weight count. At Q4_K-type layer error each
  stiff direction costs more than 3.875 bits, so 1.18 b/w is reachable only where at most 30.5% of the directions are
  stiff (`model`: worst-case covering, `ρ` as the distortion, r99 as the stiff count).
- On the three measured `ffn_down` blocks, block 3 meets that (0.110) and blocks 11 and 23 do not (0.859, 0.492). The
  same split appeared in the W13 isotropic floor (Q4_K-type `ρ` at 1 b/w on block 3 only), now from a statement that
  does not assume isotropic rows.
- Across model scale (0.5B → 8B → 32B), global reverse water-filling in the single-tensor K-FAC metric drops `R*`
  from 3.1731 → 2.2546 → 1.9927 b/w (separable), flattening near 2.0 b/w (`3.78×` Q4's KL at 1.18 b/w on 32B).
  The zero-side-information K-FAC oracle floor (`σ²_ij = C_ij²`) drops from 2.353 b/w (0.5B) to **1.5908 b/w** (8B,
  ratio 0.7061, `2.11×` Q4's KL at 1.18 b/w), projecting to `1.33–1.41` b/w at 32B. Within-tensor basis changes
  cannot move the floor (`spectrum_conj`), weight negentropy/context in the K-FAC basis is `< 0.15` b/w (`converse_w15c`),
  and block/NMF variance models buy `≤ 0.02` b/w before side information (`variance_ladder_refuted`). Reaching Q4 KL at
  1.18 b/w therefore requires (1) closing the constructive method gap (`method_gap_1133`) by combining all three faces
  (`rotation_elliptic` + `shear_parabolic` + `rebalance_hyperbolic`), decoupled prior regularization (`decoupled_ridge_opt`),
  full-attention 2-plane hierarchical scales + butterfly sign-group gauge (`had-fold-2plane-attn-bscale`: `1.8500` nats),
  the 0-side-info `E₈ ∪ O₈` spinor + parabolic-sparse lattice codebook with delayed-decision `beam=4` search and
  Marchenko-Pastur damped global 8-tuple ICM (`had-fold-2plane-attn-bscale-e8-icm20`: `1.5216` nats held-out / `77.24× Q4_K_M`,
  `0.4269` nats in-sample / `1.098×` of the `0.3887` uniform-rate separable floor), Stage-1 expansion drift attenuation
  (`had-fold-2plane-attn-bscale-e8-icm20-gin25`: `1.4974` nats held-out / `76.01× Q4_K_M`, `41.41%` top-1), and Riemannian
  `RMSNorm` pullback sink token gating + row-trust radial projection + coherence-anchored readout `fnorm`
  (`had-fold-2plane-attn-bscale-e8-icm20-gin25-sink-fnorm`: **`1.3777` nats held-out / `69.93× Q4_K_M`**, **`0.3749` nats
  in-sample / `21.55× Q4_K_M` = `0.9646×` of the `0.3887` uniform-rate separable floor**, **`42.19%` held-out top-1** at
  `1.1795` b/w), and (2) closing the finite-sample cross-layer generalization factor (`KL_out / KL_in = 3.675×`).

## Not covered

- `covering_floor` is a worst-case covering bound for a ball. A source distribution and an average distortion need
  the Shannon lower bound (`anchor`, not retrieved); the KL is a quadratic form only in the small-perturbation regime.
- The stiff count `r99` is a 99%-energy rank of the layer Gram (`G = I`), not of the full Fisher `G ⊗ H`.
- The water-filling floor assumes Gaussian components (`λ e^{−c r}` with `c = 2 ln 2` per bit) and the single-tensor
  quadratic form; W15a shows the quadratic form within 15% at one-bit error on `ffn_down` only. K-FAC drops cross-token
  terms, `g`–`x` dependence, and multi-matrix bilinear/residual coupling (`had-gptq1` under-read 0.66×).
- No byte, cycle or nat follows from a theorem here; numbers in the verdicts are measured inputs.
